Through the dating phase the running questions are whether you are acceptable to them yet, and how to get them to commit. What goes unasked is whether you like this person, whether they fit your life, whether your futures point the same way. Priebe's point is that the omission does not stay hidden — it surfaces later, as the conflict phase, once the securing is done.
