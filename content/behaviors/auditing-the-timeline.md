Stopping on the gaps and contradictions in a partner's account. From inside, this is the avoidant route to changing a feeling — reconstruct what caused it and the state becomes manageable. From the anxious side it lands as a verdict that the feeling is unearned, which is the moment the conversation stops being about the original hurt.
