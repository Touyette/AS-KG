The anxious tell for the same moderate range, and a genuinely useful diagnostic: a sudden preoccupation with whether someone is annoyed with you usually means distress has risen somewhere in your life, not necessarily in that relationship. Attention has simply been trained to go looking for closeness whenever the level climbs.
