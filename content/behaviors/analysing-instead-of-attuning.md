Priebe's own example, and an unusually candid one: an entire earlier career built on personality models, so that everyone she met could be placed on the right axes. It felt like attunement because she was attuned — to the image of the person assembled in her head. In the room she was half present, measuring what was said against the models. For someone else the frame might be good person or bad, competent or not; the effect is the same.
