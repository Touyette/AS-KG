The misuse she calls the largest online: learning someone's style in order to route around what they have said they want. Her argument is that the move is self-defeating on its own terms — attempting to override another person's stated wishes is itself an insecure response, and security is precisely the capacity to take someone at face value. She notes recovery programmes carry an explicit rule against using their own language this way, for the same reason.
