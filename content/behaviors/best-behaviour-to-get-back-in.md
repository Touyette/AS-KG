Agreeing, at the door, to the amount of space that broke you last time — and knowing in your gut that in six months it will not hold. Priebe says we always know when we are doing this, and names it as a boundary violation in its own right rather than optimism: the other person is being enrolled in a plan on terms you have privately not accepted.
