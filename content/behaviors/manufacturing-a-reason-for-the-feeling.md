If raw feeling will not be taken seriously, the anxious partner supplies a rationale that will. Priebe's example: irritation at a partner newly working from home, unexplained, gets recast as worry about money and the mortgage — and now the couple is having a detailed conversation on a level where the problem does not exist. The real one was needing time alone.
