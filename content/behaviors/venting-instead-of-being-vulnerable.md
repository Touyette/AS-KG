Priebe separates seeking support from building a case. Describing your partner as terrible so a friend will say leave them is not the vulnerable version; the vulnerable version is saying you do not know how to fix it, that you are in pain, that you are afraid of being told to leave, and that you are not ready to. The first keeps the shame intact; only the second gets you anything.
