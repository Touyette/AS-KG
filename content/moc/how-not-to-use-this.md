title: How Not To Use This
---

Priebe devotes a whole video to the misuse of attachment theory
([How Not To Use Attachment Theory](https://youtu.be/vlL6L7Cck-c)), and it is the
right frame for everything else on this site. Our own version of the warning:

**A graph makes things look more fixed than they are.** Nodes and edges render a
pattern as though it were a mechanism with settled parts. Attachment patterns are
learned, context-dependent and change with relationship and circumstance. The
structure here is a reading aid, not an anatomy.

**Styles are not types.** People move between patterns, hold different ones in
different relationships, and rarely match a description cleanly. A style is a
description of a strategy under stress, not a category of person.

**It is not a diagnosis, and it is not a verdict on anyone else.** The most common
misuse is reaching for the framework to explain a partner's behaviour rather than
one's own — a label that ends curiosity instead of starting it.

**Reading is not the work.** Nothing on this site changes a pattern. It exists to
help you find your way back to the material and see how the pieces connect.

> [!caution]
> Nothing here is therapy or a substitute for it. If you are working through
> something difficult, a qualified therapist is the right place for it.
