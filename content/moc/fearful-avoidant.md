## Does this sound like you?

You want closeness and you cannot settle into it. The pattern often peaks when things are going well — calm, open, no crisis — because that is the point at which something in you starts waiting for the turn.

You may have left relationships you then wanted back within a fortnight, and been unable to explain either move. You are hard on yourself about it, and the harshness is usually worse than anything the situation warrants.

You did not choose this. It is what a system learns when the person who was the source of safety was also, sometimes, the source of danger — and both readings were correct.

> [!tip] You can be different in different relationships
> These are descriptions of what a system does under stress, not personality types. People run differently with different people and at different times, and the point of reading one is recognition, not classification.
