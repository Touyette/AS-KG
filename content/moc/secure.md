## What this looks like

Not the absence of conflict, and not calmness under fire. Secure attachment mostly looks like taking your own distress seriously early, while there are still options — rather than being unusually composed once it is severe.

Both programs stay online: what you appreciate about someone and what the relationship costs you, at the same time, which is what makes an actual negotiation possible. Feeling and reasoning are both available at once instead of taking turns.

This page is the reference point the other three are moving toward. It is a direction, not a finish line, and nobody is at it all the time.

> [!tip] You can be different in different relationships
> These are descriptions of what a system does under stress, not personality types. People run differently with different people and at different times, and the point of reading one is recognition, not classification.
