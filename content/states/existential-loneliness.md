Not the ordinary version — nobody in my life currently sees me — but a felt certainty that being seen and known is not a thing that can happen. Priebe traces it to a sense of self that formed largely without mirroring, and compounds it: where a phobia of inner experience has left parts of you unvisited, the sense that you cannot be fully known is also accurate.
