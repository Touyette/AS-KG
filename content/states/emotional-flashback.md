The activated anxious state read as a flashback rather than a mood. Some part of the system is filing the situation exactly as it did in childhood — I cannot meet my own needs, so my only route is making my distress unmissable to this person. Technically untrue for an adult with money, friends and skills, and completely convincing from inside, because the part running it cannot access how old you are.
