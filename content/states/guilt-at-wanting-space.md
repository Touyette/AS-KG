The other arm. Enmeshment patterning says that being in a relationship means giving your partner everything and caretaking without interruption, so any move toward your own separateness registers as a moral failure. Priebe finds fearful-avoidants the most caretaking of the styles, and reads the guilt as what eventually forces the exit — the only permitted route to solitude is out.
