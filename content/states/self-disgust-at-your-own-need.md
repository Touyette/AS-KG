The mechanism underneath the layer everyone gets wrong. The fear is not that the other person will reject the disclosure — it is that disclosing triggers rejection from inside. She describes tracing this in herself during a long stretch of severe pain: the moment someone's face began to shift toward concern, shame flooded in, and she would say whatever ended it. What reads from outside as being distant is a person being repelled by their own visible need.
