The long-run result of a brain that stopped attending to feelings it found useless. It often becomes visible only at a death or a breakup, when the person can see that a strong feeling is warranted and cannot locate it in themselves. She is explicit that this is not an absence of empathy — it is insensitivity to one's own signal, not other people's.
