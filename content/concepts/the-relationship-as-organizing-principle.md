It is reasonable for a serious relationship to be one of the things your life orbits. The blindspot is how early that happens — three or four dates in, mood already keyed to their messages, life already rearranging. Priebe's objection is structural: two people with strong centres of gravity elsewhere have something to look into and learn about. Abandon yours and there is nothing to see.
