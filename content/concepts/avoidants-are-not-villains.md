Popular attachment writing tends to sympathise with the anxious style and warn people away from the avoidant one. Priebe rejects that split: the avoidant adaptation is as involuntary and as early as the anxious one, and describing it as a character flaw makes it harder for avoidant people to recognise themselves in the material at all.
