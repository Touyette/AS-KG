After a breakup the avoidant machinery reports that nothing is wrong: it meant little, I am fine, I am strong. Then a crisis or a chance meeting arrives and the whole thing lands at once. Priebe traces the deficit to the original wound — the source of the pain was the attachment figure, so the pain had to be concealed from the only person who could have helped process it, and grieving was never learned.
