A preference either way is fine; what marks security is that the sense of self does not change much between the two, and that being partnered does not require hiding any part of it. Priebe adds the practical consequence — secure people compromise, but choose relationships where the compromises improve the life, and leave early when the values plainly do not match rather than spending years on it.
