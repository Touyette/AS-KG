Priebe's account of why the pairing is so reliable. A good parent does not burden a small child with their problems, so the avoidant's aura of having nothing going on reads, to an anxious nervous system, as exactly the figure it has been looking for — more so than a secure person visibly working through their own material. The problem is that the parent is not a parent and has no intention of endlessly meeting needs.
