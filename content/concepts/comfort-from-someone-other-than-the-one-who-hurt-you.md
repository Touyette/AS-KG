The wound underneath fearful-avoidance is having needed comfort from the person causing the distress, with no alternative available. The belief that follows is that only the source of the pain can relieve it. Priebe treats breaking that specific link — learning to seek real support outside the relationship the trouble is in — as the single highest-leverage move available to the style.
