Priebe stops to disambiguate the word. She does not mean becoming easily wounded or more defended. She means literally being in your senses: noticing what is happening in reality right now, how it is landing in your body, and what is shifting in the other person's energy as the conversation moves. It is the same faculty a dancer uses to follow a partner's small changes.
