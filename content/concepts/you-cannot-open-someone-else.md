Her closing note, addressed to the partners rather than the person. Reassurance that you will not reject them does not reach the mechanism, because the rejection is internal. She adds an uncomfortable point: the patterns exist because they worked, and prising them open in someone who has not chosen the work is not obviously a kindness.
