If you want to end it but also want to stay friends, keep seeing them, keep them in reach — Priebe reads that as a signal you are not ready to leave. You are overwhelmed, and ending the relationship is the only route to space you have. The need is real; the instrument is wrong. What is missing is the ability to ask for autonomy while staying in.
