A running background calculation in every relationship — friendship, work, romance — of what each person wants and what each has to withhold. Priebe notes it usually never surfaces as a thought; it shows up as who you can relax around. Attraction with uncertain reciprocity is uncomfortable for exactly this reason: you want their approval and they do not need yours, so the position has to be improved before anything can be risked.
