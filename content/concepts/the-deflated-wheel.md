Her frame for why reflection after a bad relationship so often fails. Reviewing what went wrong, we reach for the thing we already do well and do more of it — inflating a tyre that was half full while the flat one stays flat, because it sits in the blind spot. The result is a bike that steers only in circles. Each insecure style has a different over-used strength and a different missing one.
