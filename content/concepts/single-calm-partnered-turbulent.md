The avoidant spectrum is often steadier single than partnered, because closeness is what brings the wounds to the surface. The anxious spectrum runs the opposite way — being alone is when the material arrives. Priebe notes the practical consequence: the avoidant's post-breakup relief is real and is not evidence that the relationship meant nothing.
