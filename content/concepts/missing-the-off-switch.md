The anxious counterpart. The pain registers fully; what is missing is the mechanism that ends it. Priebe's image is a stubbed toe that keeps hurting, harder and harder, until somebody else arrives to stop it — at which point wanting someone permanently within reach stops looking like insecurity and starts looking like a reasonable precaution.
