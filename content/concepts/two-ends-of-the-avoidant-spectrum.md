At the mild end, negative emotion was merely unrewarding, and the adult reroutes to problem-solving without noticing. At the severe end it was dangerous, and the adult suppresses both unconsciously and deliberately, may resent other people's freedom to express, and can carry an ego about enduring without complaint — while almost never naming what was done to them as abuse.
