Participants awaiting intermittent electric shocks were measured with and without another person present holding their hand. Anxious participants' stress fell when someone was there; avoidant participants' stress rose. Priebe reports it from Attached or The Attachment Effect and reads the avoidant result as the cost of having a second nervous system to manage.
