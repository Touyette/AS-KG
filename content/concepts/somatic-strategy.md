The attachment strategy is laid down in the body before language exists. If extreme distress met disgust or a caregiver going distant, the body begins suppressing extreme distress. If only extreme distress got a response, the body begins amplifying discomfort. Priebe's emphasis is that nobody selected this — the secure belief that showing a feeling at its true volume brings appropriate care is simply not what your tissue learned.
