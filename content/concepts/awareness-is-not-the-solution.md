A correction Priebe returns to across her work and states plainly here: people reaching stage two assume that because they can see the problem they should be able to solve it, and conclude something is wrong with them when they cannot. Seeing it and being able to do otherwise are separate acquisitions, and the gap between them is the healing process rather than a failure inside it.
