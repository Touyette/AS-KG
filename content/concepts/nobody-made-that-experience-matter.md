A phrase Priebe took from a speaker at a workshop, describing a close family member dying in childhood: nobody made that experience matter. It is the caregiver's job to see the child's interior and connect it to what has happened — you feel grief, you feel loss, those are the right responses to this. Where that does not happen, the feeling arrives with no place to sit.
