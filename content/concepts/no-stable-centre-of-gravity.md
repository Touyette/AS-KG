Priebe puts numbers on it. The anxious person sits at roughly eighty per cent outside themselves and twenty per cent grounded; the avoidant runs the inverse; the fearful-avoidant hovers near the middle with no settled answer. Hence the practical paralysis — no stable view on whether to invest in relationships or in self-work, because whichever you chose last week was chosen by a different configuration.
