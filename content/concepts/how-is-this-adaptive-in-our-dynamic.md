Insecure couples argue about who is right; secure ones have a working sense of context. Priebe's replacement question, asked together: how could this behaviour be adaptive inside the relationship we have built? Explosions may be what a person resorts to when the low-volume version is never integrated. Withdrawal may be what happens when parts of someone get quietly declined every time they arrive.
