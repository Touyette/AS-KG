Emotions are what tell you what matters, so losing access to them breaks decision-making at both scales. At the large scale you follow other people's formula for a good life, hit the milestones, and cannot work out why arriving feels like nothing. At the small scale you can spend an hour on dinner or a shirt, because with no guiding principles the only available signal is the next small hit.
