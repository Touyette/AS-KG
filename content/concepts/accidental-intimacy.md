The fearful-avoidant keeps the shame-bound material — which is to say, ordinary human emotion — out of every social and professional setting, and it surfaces only where self-control fails: inside a close relationship, under trigger. So the one experience of being fully known arrives as a by-product of the worst moments, and belongs exclusively to the relationship that produced them.
