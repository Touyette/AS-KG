Why the usual personality-system habits do not transfer. Those frameworks assume the type is fixed, so the useful move is to celebrate your quirks and work with your natural energy. Attachment styles are changeable, and what is dominant in an insecure one is mostly the part that is not working — so treating it as an identity to embrace inverts the point of knowing it.
