The rule Priebe says she has re-recorded this video repeatedly to get right. Every style borrows behaviour from every other: anxious people feel avoidant in the wrong relationship, avoidant people feel anxious about losing the one arrangement that lets them function as they prefer, secure people feel both when the situation warrants. Typing by behaviour, or even by feeling, gets you nowhere. The motivation underneath is the only thing that separates them.
