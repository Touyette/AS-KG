First level: irritated, overloaded. Second level: I cannot handle this much emotion, and that makes me uncomfortable, inadequate, possibly frightened. Underneath sits a need — time to think, or someone to ask the right questions. Priebe's point is that only the second level and the need are communicable; the first level on its own gives the other person nothing to work with.
