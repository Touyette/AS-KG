Priebe notes the gap between the popular picture of anxious attachment — sympathetic, deprived of love — and what the literature actually describes, which is controlling. Not usually overt: the control is over how the partner feels, so that they stay aligned and connected and the co-regulation stays available. She adds that you would be hard pressed to find an anxious person who recognises it, because it runs below the level of choice.
