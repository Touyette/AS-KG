Fearful-avoidant is the category people place themselves in when they can see both anxious and avoidant behaviour in themselves but cannot yet tell the two apart. Secure people land there too, because a secure person does feel anxious when there is something to be anxious about. The discriminator is not the mix of behaviours but whether they fire when the situation warrants them.
