Procrastinating at work, picking fights, eating when you are not hungry — apparently unrelated until you notice they all run when you feel insecure. Priebe's argument for the slowness of stage two is that noticing across enough areas is what lets you find the root, and that solving the root retires the symptoms without having to be fought one at a time.
