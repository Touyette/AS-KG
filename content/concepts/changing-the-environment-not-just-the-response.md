Half of the work is behaving securely in the situations you are already in. The other half is noticing which situations you selected because the old strategy handles them, and moving. Priebe's line is that you cannot get healthy while unprotected in a sick environment — trying to be secure where insecure responses are genuinely the most adaptive is not a fair test of anything.
