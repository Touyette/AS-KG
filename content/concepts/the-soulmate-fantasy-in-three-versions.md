Anxious: someone who loves the flaws away and completes a life. Avoidant: someone who has a normal relationship with you while staying perfectly regulated, never unreasonable, never asking for anything unwanted, and intuiting everything so nothing needs saying. Fearful-avoidant: a soulmate who exists, for whom you are not yet good enough, so you develop quietly until you qualify. Priebe calls all three bogus in the same breath.
