Total dependency is a problem; so is refusing to rely on anyone for anything. Priebe puts security in the middle: trusting people to do what they said, outsourcing accordingly, and renegotiating when it stops working. It rests on explicit agreements and on taking people at face value about what they can and cannot do — which does not prevent being let down, only makes it legible when it happens.
