Priebe's image for the correction. The soup is plentiful and freely given; what differs is whether you arrived with a bowl. The anxious spectrum has no vessel and must stand under the ladle or borrow someone else's. The avoidant has the bowl but was once served something poisoned, so the soup gets carried around uneaten. In both cases supply was never the problem.
