Each insecure style is moving toward something it never arrives at. The anxious person is going for relationship security and keeps finding another floor below the one they reached. The avoidant person is going for complete self-sufficiency. The fearful-avoidant has no settled direction at all — approach and exit alternate — which is why the pattern is disorienting from the inside as much as from outside.
