The two things attuned care supplies, and the distinction that organises the whole model. Protection resolves the cause so it is less likely to recur, and includes the unwelcome limits that prevent future pain; comfort meets the fact that some of it is hard regardless. Each insecure style turns out to have received one and not the other.
