Securely attached people frequently type themselves as insecure, because a settled nervous system can afford to look squarely at what a parent got wrong. The avoidant adaptation runs the other way — the childhood has to be described as fine. So an unflattering account of your parents is weak evidence of insecure attachment, and a flawless one is weak evidence of secure attachment.
