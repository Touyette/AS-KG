Priebe stops to rule this out explicitly. An infant lacks the equipment for manipulation; it is running trial and error and keeping whatever works. If expressing the real feeling was punished and expressing a different one was rewarded, the child learns the substitution without ever forming the intention. The same behaviour in an adult, with adult capacities, is another matter.
