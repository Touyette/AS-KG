Believing the partner is not competent to help means never bringing them an unfinished problem — so the work is done alone and delivered as a conclusion. Priebe describes a partner who, after their first argument, produced a long letter explaining why he needed to step well back; her reply was, why not just talk about it and find a new balance. He looked at her as though she were speaking another language, and then they did exactly that.
