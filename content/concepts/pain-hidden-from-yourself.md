Her correction of the usual stereotype, and an important one: this is not someone concealing pain from other people. When it reaches conscious awareness they process it about as well as anyone. The trouble is that it largely does not arrive — frustration and contempt surface in its place, and once out of the activating moment the memory loses most of its emotional charge. Better for analysis, useless for learning.
