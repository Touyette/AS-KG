Attachment is built partly by both people pursuing — making plans, making the other feel wanted, rearranging around them. The avoidant tendency is to let the other person do the fitting-in. Priebe's rule of thumb: how guarded you are sets how unbounded a partner you attract, because a secure person reads no room in your life and withdraws, while someone whose worth depends on your approval will step over their own limits to stay.
