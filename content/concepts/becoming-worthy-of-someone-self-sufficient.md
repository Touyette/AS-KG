The anxious appraisal of a new person is an audit of what they have that you lack — charm, confidence, ease — and a private conclusion that you will never have it, but could acquire it by being loved by someone who does. Hence the paradox: real investment in self-improvement, aimed not at becoming self-sufficient but at becoming the sort of person a self-sufficient person would choose.
