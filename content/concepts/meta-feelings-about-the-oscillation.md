Fearful-avoidance is not only anxious triggers plus avoidant triggers. Because the system keeps switching, each state passes judgment on the other: the deactivated self is ashamed of what the activated self felt and said, and the activated self feels guilty about the distance the deactivated self wanted. Priebe treats this second layer as the distinctively fearful-avoidant part.
