Storming out, picking a fight and threatening to end it appear in both the anxious and the avoidant repertoire, and they mean opposite things. Run as protest, the behaviour is a bid for pursuit — the hoped-for outcome is being followed. Run as deactivation, the hoped-for outcome is distance. Read the wanted result, not the action, or you will type yourself and your partner wrongly.
