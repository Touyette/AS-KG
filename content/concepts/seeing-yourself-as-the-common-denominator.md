The fearful-avoidant does eventually notice they are the constant across their relationships — which is more than most insecure styles manage — and then draws the wrong inference from it, because they have no account of their own mechanism. Priebe's counter-claim is that what looks like bad character is early wiring for self-protection, and that the well-meaning part underneath is what healing is grown from.
