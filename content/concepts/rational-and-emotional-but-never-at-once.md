The fearful-avoidant has access to distorted emotion like the anxious and distorted logic like the avoidant, and no integration between them. Priebe describes the effect on the people around them: a friend who can set out precisely why the relationship cannot work, and keeps choosing it — because the analysis was done deactivated and the decisions are made activated, and neither state can reach the other.
