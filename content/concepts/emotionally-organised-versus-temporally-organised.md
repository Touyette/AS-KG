Priebe's account of why the same argument reads so differently from each side. The anxious system organises reality around felt experience: what is true about this situation is what I am feeling. The avoidant system organises it around sequence: what is true is the order of events that produced the state. Neither is lying. They are indexing reality on different keys, and each reads the other's key as an evasion.
