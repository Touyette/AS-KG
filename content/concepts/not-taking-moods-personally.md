There are two ways to take a mood personally: reading it as rejection, or being irritated that someone is displaying what you have learned to suppress. Priebe's secure version is neither — curiosity at a slight distance, an offer of help if it is within reach, and otherwise carrying on, because their state genuinely is not about you.
