How a child learns to categorise its own inner states: something happens, and it checks the faces around it. Frightened, but everyone is eating popcorn — so this kind of frightening is safe. The mechanism only works if the room's response maps onto the child's interior. In an avoidant family where the grief is felt and not shown, the child concludes the grief itself is the wrong thing to be feeling.
