Setting a goal that means something needs both faculties: the emotional one that knows what speaks to you, and the practical one that knows what makes sense. Priebe's marker is not that secure people never get this wrong, but that they do not have a lifelong structural weakness at either end — they can direct their own life and merge it with someone else's, and count both kinds of goal as real.
