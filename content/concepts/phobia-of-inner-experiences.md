Certain emotional states have no regulation attached to them, so the system redirects before they arrive. Priebe's examples are specific: anger begins in the body and a freeze response takes over; sadness begins and attention suddenly has to be somewhere else, online, researching, anywhere. It reads as distraction. It is a flight response, and the information in the feeling never lands.
