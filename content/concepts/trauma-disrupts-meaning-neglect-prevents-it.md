Priebe's cleanest distinction, taken from a joke she saw about someone with complex trauma listening to someone with single-incident trauma describe life before it — wait, you had a before? Single-incident trauma disrupts an existing system of meaning. Neglect interferes with forming one at all: with knowing how the world works and what deserves your attention and investment.
