The fearful-avoidant runs both patterns and so has both jobs: find where the pain receptors are muted and sensitize to what is being felt but not registered, and learn to regulate in the areas where the pain is already loud and constant. Priebe adds that the identity confusion — am I the tough one or the needy one — is itself a symptom of running both.
