Rating anger one to ten and noticing where you actually speak. Priebe's own pattern was silence at everything up to eight and then a nine or ten, at which point self-regulation is no longer available and the conversation cannot go well. The workable band is low — around two or three, when the feeling is small enough to state calmly and the problem is small enough to fix.
