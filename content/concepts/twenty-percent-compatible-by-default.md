Priebe's diagnosis of the avoidant dating pattern: fear of rejection means rarely pursuing anyone actually wanted, so the field narrows to whoever approaches — which is disproportionately people who cannot meet their own needs. The complaint about always dating people who need too much is therefore a report on a selection method, not on the world. Nobody is a hundred per cent match; eighty is available to someone who shows up.
