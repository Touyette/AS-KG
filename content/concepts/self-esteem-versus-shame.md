Self-esteem is holding that you are well-meaning and that you get things wrong, both at once — not the conviction that you never do, which she distinguishes sharply as something else. Someone shame-bound believes they are fundamentally a mistake, and can carry a strong ego with no self-esteem at all, which is why they cannot afford to be wrong about anything.
