To avoid a thing you have to know it is there. Priebe's objection to the label is that the dismissive type is mostly not declining to look — the painful thought does not reach awareness at all. Conscious avoidance of distressing thoughts is more characteristic of the anxious and fearful-avoidant types. Her own names would be closer to oblivious and obsessive, which she concedes would not catch on.
