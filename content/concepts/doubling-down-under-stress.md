Ambiguity and external threat are precisely when co-regulation is worth most, and precisely when both parties revert hardest — the avoidant to control or detachment, the anxious to fixation, each aimed at the other. Priebe's alternative is not agreement but alliance: this situation is hard, our needs differ, neither of us has to be shamed for that, and it helps to have someone to hold on to.
