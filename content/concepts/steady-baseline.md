Being alright as a constant rather than an event. Not overwhelming, which is why it can be mistaken for dullness, and precisely why it is not chased. Her contrast is standing on solid ground versus standing on a summit that might give way: what is real stays steady whether things are going well or badly, and only a fantasy that happens to match reality has to be defended against changing.
