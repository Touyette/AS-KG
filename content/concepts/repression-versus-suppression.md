Suppression is knowing a need is there, choosing to set it down for now, and intending to come back to it — renting while you save for the house you know you want. Repression puts the need somewhere you cannot find it, so you keep moving and feel vaguely wrong without ever locating what you are missing. Both look like going without; only one leaves the need retrievable.
