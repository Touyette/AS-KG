A child has a survival claim on a parent's attention, and the tactics that enforce that claim are legitimate at four. An adult partner has no such obligation: they may decide not to care, not to love you, and to walk away in the moment you need them. Priebe states it without softening, because the tactics only make sense while the claim is assumed.
