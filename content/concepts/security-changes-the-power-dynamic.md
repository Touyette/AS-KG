A secure relationship has a roughly even power balance, so moving toward one means the existing balance goes. If you want your partner more independent, you will be the one reaching out, planning, fitting into their life. If you want them more emotional, you will be holding raw dysregulated material from someone who has suppressed it for decades. Priebe's rule: be ready for the side effects, or your system will quietly push them back into the old role.
