Activated, the fearful-avoidant is owed things and the partner is failing them. Deactivated, they cannot reach the emotion they were in, look at their own conduct through a cold analytic lens and conclude they must be the abusive one. Priebe is careful on both sides: they genuinely can be, as any insecure style can, and the deactivated verdict is not more accurate for being calmer. The disorientation is the diagnostic feature.
