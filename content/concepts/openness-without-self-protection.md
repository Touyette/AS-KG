The anxious imbalance. She is careful to name the strength first and rate it highly: a real openness to being taught and changed by loving someone, which she says is genuinely unavailable to much of the avoidant spectrum and is required for a secure partnership. Unbalanced it leaves a person entirely undefended — her image is an infant lying open-armed in a cot, an energy that works precisely because someone else is doing the protecting.
