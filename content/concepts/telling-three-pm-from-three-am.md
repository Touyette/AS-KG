Her image for the whole problem. Crossing a park is sensible at three in the afternoon and unwise at three in the morning; the same fear is rational or irrational depending on which it is. Someone who cannot tell the time will either take the long way around every time or walk straight in at the wrong hour — and neither is a fear problem, it is a reading problem.
