Both anxious and fearful-avoidant drive the same road — my needs get met by someone else — but the fearful-avoidant reads every exit sign, believing the turn-off leads to a place where their own needs are permitted again. So the fantasy is not a scene in which the partner runs after them; it is the relationship genuinely over and life good afterwards. Believed long enough, the relationship becomes the explanation for everything missing.
