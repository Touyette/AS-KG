Terms Priebe takes from a friend, for the two halves of a projection. The signal is the true thing inside what is being said; the muddle is everything inaccurate around it. "You don't care about me" is muddle to the person receiving it and signal to the person saying it. Neither of them is wrong, and nothing resolves until both are willing to dig for the kernel together rather than argue whose reading wins.
