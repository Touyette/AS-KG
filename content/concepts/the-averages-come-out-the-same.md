Looking back at a three-year on-and-off relationship, Priebe worked out that the total time she spent alone versus with her partner came out at roughly what a secure couple would have anyway. The difference was distribution: secure people notice they need space on a Tuesday and take it, while enmeshment followed by a breakup takes the same solitude in one indigestible piece, at the cost of the relationship.
