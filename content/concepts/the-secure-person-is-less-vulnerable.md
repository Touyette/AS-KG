The counterintuitive comparison Priebe puts to the avoidant reader. A secure person is less exposed to hurt, because they have people who help them recover from it; less exposed to betrayal, because they engage honestly with others doing the same; less exposed to rejection, because they can be seen whole rather than through a persona. The wall is not the safer position, it only feels like it.
