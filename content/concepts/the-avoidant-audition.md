Avoidant courtship runs long and reads as evaluation rather than falling: how does this person behave here, what do they like, what do they earn, do I respect it — and a firm boundary set partly to see whether it is honoured. Attraction is one of ten or twenty inputs. Priebe is careful that this is not absence of feeling; it is feeling at low volume under a permanent sedative.
