Commitments get made sincerely by one configuration and come due to another — the career built on emotional openness that becomes unbearable the week vulnerability is actually required, the resolution to be emotionally healthy that dissolves on the next activation. Priebe's point is that the resulting self-distrust is well-founded, and that awareness of the pattern makes decisions harder before it makes them easier.
