Her central move in this video, built on a pair of images. Live with a low-grade headache long enough and painkillers start to feel sublime — but the best available state was never the pill, it was not having the headache, and you have no way to tell the difference from inside. Live hungry and food becomes the whole world; eat properly and your attention is free for everything else. The highs of insecure attachment are relief from a chronic background of not being good enough, and relief is temporary by construction.
