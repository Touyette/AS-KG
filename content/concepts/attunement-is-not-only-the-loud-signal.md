Priebe's answer to the standard objection — that attending to your feelings would make you irrational and idle. She borrows the intuitive-eating case: the fear is that you would live on pizza, but genuine attunement includes noticing which foods leave you flat and which are easy on you. Emotionally the same holds. The system that says stay in bed is also the system that wants to feel proud of itself and will supply the discipline.
