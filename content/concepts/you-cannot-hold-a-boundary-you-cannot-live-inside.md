Priebe's explanation for why anxiously attached people are the most aware they need boundaries and the least able to keep them. A boundary is a space you must then self-regulate inside — and the thing they regulate with is on the other side of it. So the boundary goes up, the dysregulation arrives, and the boundary comes straight back down. It is not weakness of will; it is the design.
