The shift from experiencing a reaction as evidence about who you are to experiencing it as something running in you. She flags it as unusually load-bearing for fearful-avoidant people, whose default reading is that they are simply bad or incapable of love rather than conditioned — and it is the move that makes the rest of the work survivable.
