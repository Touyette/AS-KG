A partner who finds you flawless confirms the I-am-okay-you-are-not model, and is therefore easy to be around — and cannot tolerate it when you eventually bring something real. Meanwhile a secure partner noticing where you are struggling registers as criticism, or as being found insufficient, when it is usually just attention. Priebe's ask is to get comfortable being seen whole, unfinished parts included.
