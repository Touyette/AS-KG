The step Priebe rates most important and least expected: work out what need the behaviour you want gone is currently meeting. Constant screaming matches can be what holds a couple together if it is the only relationship model either of them has; explosive fights can be the only route by which your needs ever get seen. Where there is a payoff, dropping the behaviour meets fierce resistance from a part of you that is not in the conversation.
