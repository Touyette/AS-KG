One account of fearful-avoidant development that Priebe finds useful: a single base strategy — say, avoidant — with trauma triggers that periodically flip the system into its opposite. Downplay everything most of the time; then a flashback arrives and the response overshoots into amplification or aggression. Run often enough, it stops looking like intrusions and starts looking like a style.
