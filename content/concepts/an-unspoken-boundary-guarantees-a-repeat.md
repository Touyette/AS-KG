Slipping away, going quiet and hoping the other person works it out leaves them with no information, so the next attempt comes with more force — and the situation recurs at higher intensity. Priebe's structure for the avoidant is that the end of the sequence is right (go and regulate alone) and the middle is missing: say what is wrong, what is unacceptable, and what would need to change.
