Priebe calls this the single biggest misunderstanding in attachment theory. Having both anxious and avoidant traits does not make you fearful-avoidant, because everybody has both. What makes the style its own thing is a chronic disorientation about direction — am I going toward love or toward independence — combined with distrust of yourself and of everyone else at the same time.
