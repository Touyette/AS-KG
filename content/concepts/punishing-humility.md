A partner arrives with an apology and gets the other ten unaddressed items, or a speech about how unacceptable it all was. Understandable — humility from them is rare, so the window feels precious — and it reliably ensures the next one does not come. Priebe's wider version: an environment hostile to mistakes does not produce fewer mistakes, it produces less honesty about them.
