Where a caregiver answered vulnerability or a wish for closeness with subtle disgust, the pairing gets learned the way any conditioned response does. Avoiding intimacy was then the adaptive move — it kept the reaction from being seen — and the association survives into adulthood attached to people who would never produce it.
