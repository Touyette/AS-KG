Priebe's improv story makes the point better than the theory does. A friendship had gone flat while she deferred to what the other person wanted; in a class exercise where each character held a private objective, the interplay came straight back. Wanting something that had nothing to do with his approval was what put energy between them. Intimacy needs edges — otherwise nobody can tell where one of you stops.
