Secure people generally do not enjoy conflict and are willing to have it, because they either saw it resolved peacefully or learned later that it can be. Priebe's sharpest observation is about what the insecure styles are missing: not the courage but the knowledge — at a gut level they do not know that a direct conversation which simply settles something is an available outcome.
