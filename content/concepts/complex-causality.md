The security position, and the exit from the blame see-saw: most conflicts are produced by two people acting in good faith whose triggers and unexamined programmes collide. Not because either is malicious, but because communicating across two histories is difficult and needs continuous renegotiation. Priebe calls the alternative — one of you is doing it all wrong — a false dichotomy in both directions.
