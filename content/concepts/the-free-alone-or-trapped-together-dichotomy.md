The belief the fearful-avoidant most needs dismantled: that autonomy and connection are alternatives you pick between. Priebe's counter is that the freedom claimed by leaving — time alone, friendships, solo holidays, a normal degree of independence — is all available inside a relationship, and was only unavailable in the enmeshed family the belief came from.
