Mid-argument, the question worth asking is which goal is running. Advocating a need and hearing theirs is one thing; getting them to make you feel better is another, and the second quietly replaces the first without announcing itself. It sounds like: how do I get them to see how much pain I am in. The points you came in with are usually the casualty.
