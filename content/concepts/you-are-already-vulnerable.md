The challenger position starts from the fact that you are affected by every person and situation you engage with, whether or not you concede it. Priebe's line to the avoidant reader is that closing off does not remove the feelings, it only routes them out sideways — so given that you are exposed either way, you may as well pick an exposure that takes you somewhere you want to be.
