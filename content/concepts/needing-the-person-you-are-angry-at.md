At the top of the scale the fearful-avoidant system runs two incompatible programmes at once: get the anger out, and get this person on side so they will help bring me down again. Priebe traces it to never having learned down-regulation from anger in childhood — the only route back to baseline runs through someone else, and the only person available is the one being shouted at.
