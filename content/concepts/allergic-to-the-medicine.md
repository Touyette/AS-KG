The avoidant needs love as much as anyone and reacts badly to it in quantity, so it has to be taken in small amounts and spaced out. That is the shape of the slow approach — a little closeness, then distance to manage the reaction, then a little more — repeated for years. Intimacy is the trigger because intimacy once meant your boundaries were about to go.
