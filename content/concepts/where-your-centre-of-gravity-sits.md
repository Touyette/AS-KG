Priebe's somatic map of the three states, learned from having run all of them. Secure: centred in yourself, aware of your own interior and of the people around you at the same time. Deactivated: centred so far inside your own body that everyone has to be pushed out of orbit before you can relax. Activated: centred inside somebody else — the part of you that knows how to make you okay is in them, so they have to be got back on side first.
