Why a fearful-avoidant need arrives with a deadline attached. Some part of the system knows the need is normally repressed, so its brief appearance in awareness feels like the only chance it will get. The urgency is not about the partner's response time; it is about the closing window, which is why postponing feels like abandonment of the need rather than management of it.
