After a rupture the instinct is to disown the whole episode, feeling included. Priebe insists on the split: apologise for how you expressed it and for whatever was unkind or out of integrity, and keep the need that produced it, which still has to be resolved somewhere. The feeling is a normal consequence of being alive; the action is what accountability attaches to.
