Permanent openness means being permanently receptive to whatever the other person is carrying, so a short reply or a distracted evening lands as a verdict. Priebe's point is that this quietly requires your partner never to be off — and that the alternative is the skill avoidant people over-develop and secure people hold at the right amount: sitting with someone in their own difficulty and knowing it is not about you.
