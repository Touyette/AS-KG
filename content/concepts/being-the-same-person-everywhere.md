Her stated end point for fearful-avoidant healing. The test she offers is concrete: how a partner would describe you should broadly match how friends, family and colleagues would. Her image for the mechanism is oxygen — parts of us kept shut away go strange in the dark and emerge strangely; exposed a little at a time to people who can be trusted with them, they grow into something ordinary.
