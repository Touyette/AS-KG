A caregiver is asked to hold a blank, unresponsive face at an infant. The infant escalates through a ladder of bids — crying, then charming, then throwing things — because it has no other way to restore contact. Priebe uses it as the clearest picture of where protest behaviour comes from: not manipulation, but a repertoire being run through in order.
