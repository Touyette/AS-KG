The other account: at the age the strategy formed, the source of danger and the only available protection were the same person. The system cannot resolve approach against withdrawal, so it freezes, then alternates according to how the caregiver is behaving that hour. Priebe is emphatic that this is the correct response to that situation, not a malfunction.
