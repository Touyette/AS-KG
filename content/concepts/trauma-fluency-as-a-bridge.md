Priebe's argument for trauma work beyond symptom relief: it gives you words for your own history that you can hand to someone without feeling skinned in the process. Once the account can be contained and offered rather than exposed, connection stops requiring a matching history — which is what makes the world at large habitable rather than something to shelter from inside one relationship.
