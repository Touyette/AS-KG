Without reliable feedback about what keeps you in connection, anything that works seven times in ten deserves to be run every single time — it is the best available bet. That is why insecure relating goes rigid, and why the other three times in ten keep producing the same damage. Priebe's point is that the rigidity is intelligent, and that it stops being intelligent once feedback becomes available.
