Having learned early that what people say is unreliable, the fearful-avoidant reads everything else — and gets genuinely good at it. Priebe knows one who became a world-class poker player on exactly that skill. What distinguishes it from anxious hypervigilance is comfort looking at their own manipulations, which makes other people's legible too. The blind spot is secure people, whose lack of strategy reads as a strategy being concealed.
