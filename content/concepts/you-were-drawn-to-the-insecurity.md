The stoicism and self-containment that first appealed is avoidant patterning. The pursuit and the constant validation that felt so good is anxious patterning. Priebe's uncomfortable corollary: asking your partner to become secure is asking them to lose the quality that drew you in — and you may not, on inspection, want the version of them that results.
