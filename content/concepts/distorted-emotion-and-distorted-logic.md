The clean statement of the mirror. Under pressure anxious patterning trusts the feeling and arranges reasoning to fit it; avoidant patterning trusts the reasoning and arranges feeling to fit that. Neither is available to the other in the moment, which is why one partner offering to correct the other's logic reads as a refusal to believe them.
