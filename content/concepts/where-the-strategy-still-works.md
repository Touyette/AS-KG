The areas producing visible chaos are the easy ones — the pain supplies the motivation. The hard ones are where the insecure strategy is currently delivering: the job in a dishonest industry where suspicion of everyone is an asset, the relationship where a persona keeps the peace. Priebe's claim is that these are the areas requiring the most work, and the ones nobody is inclined to touch.
