Whatever reaches conscious attention does get dealt with, and strong feeling rarely arrives in the first place, so the self-assessment comes back as fine. The reading is not careless — it is accurate about everything the person can see. The style is defined by what falls outside that, which is precisely what cannot be audited from the inside.
