Differentiation is two things: knowing where you end and someone else begins, and understanding how the two of you act on each other. Avoidant patterning tends to have the first and lack the second — so a partner's difficulty reads as entirely theirs. Priebe adds the mirror-image cost: not noticing how others affect you leaves you assuming everyone else operates in a sealed compartment too.
