Out of a relationship the fearful-avoidant runs the avoidant programme and it works — comfortable, self-sufficient, apparently doing the work. Priebe's warning is that minimising your own and everyone else's emotions is not self-soothing, and it holds only until something genuinely painful arrives. Then, with no real regulation skills built, the return is straight back into enmeshment.
