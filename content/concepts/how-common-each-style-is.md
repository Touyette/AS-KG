Roughly half to sixty per cent of people are securely attached; anxious and avoidant each account for something near twenty per cent; fearful-avoidant is the rarest by a wide margin. Priebe cites the base rates specifically as a check on self-typing — the styles people most often claim are not the ones most people have.
