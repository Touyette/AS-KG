The anxious version of fairness runs: if what I want is reasonable, they ought to provide it. Priebe declines the inference. Your need can be entirely reasonable and this person can still be unwilling — and what you hold is not a claim on them but the choice of who you are with. If the pattern repeats across partners, the question moves to how much you are routing through one relationship.
