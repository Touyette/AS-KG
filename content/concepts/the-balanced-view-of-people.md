Ask a secure person about a parent or a partner and you get neither idealisation nor prosecution — the quirks that irritate them, and the circumstances that made someone unable to show up. Priebe names it as the strongest tell of earned security: someone who was genuinely abused and can now hold what happened both emotionally and rationally, without needing a villain, and without saying it was acceptable.
