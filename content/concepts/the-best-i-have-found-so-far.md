A few unsatisfying relationships later, the old one starts to look like the high-water mark. Priebe's reading is that the search was constrained rather than exhausted — nothing better turned up partly because there is no life outside a relationship for a better partner to join. The test she offers is direct: would this person add to a life that is already good, or are they a raft in a sea you are drowning in?
