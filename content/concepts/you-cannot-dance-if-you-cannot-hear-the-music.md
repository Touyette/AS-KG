Priebe's image for what analysis alone cannot do. You can learn every step and still look wrong if you cannot hear the music. She notes how often an avoidant partner in a heated conflict is not looking at the other person at all — eyes off to one side, working out the logic — which is well-intentioned and is half of what repair takes. The other half is being in front of them, feeling alongside them, responding as it happens.
