A test Priebe uses on her own anger: if the problem in front of you were solved right now, would the anger stop? If not, it is not a response to this situation, and expressing it here will not discharge it. She treats that as the marker separating a trauma trigger from genuine present-tense anger, which is worth expressing and does resolve.
