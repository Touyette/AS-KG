What Priebe identifies as the real difference between the secure and the anxious childhood: not whether the child was soothed, but whether anyone was seen handling their own distress. Secure children watch a parent go into discomfort and come out of it, and acquire the sequence. Being comforted without ever seeing that leaves you able to be regulated and unable to do it yourself.
