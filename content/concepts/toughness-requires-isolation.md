If vulnerability has to stay unreachable, relationships have to stay shallow, because give-and-take needs you to know and be able to say what you need. Priebe reads toughness as a permanently activated state — on guard, because nothing else will catch you — and strength as a calm one, because the load is spread across enough people and systems that no single failure is fatal.
