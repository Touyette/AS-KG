Get the commitment, then work out the rest. Priebe describes what usually arrives instead: the discovery that the futures do not align, protest behaviour escalating to pull the partner toward what you want, and a partner who reasonably feels misled — because the yes-to-everything person of the early months was not a person they were being shown.
