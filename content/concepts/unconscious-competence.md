The new response has become the default and no longer competes with the old one. Priebe describes it in concrete terms — safe in your own body, noticing feelings as they arrive, self-soothing without deciding to, reading trustworthiness without effortful analysis — and names it as the point at which the internal working model has actually changed rather than been overridden.
