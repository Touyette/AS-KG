Friendship intimacy, where you can be plainly imperfect. Romantic intimacy, including building a life alongside someone. Mentorship intimacy, one-directional, where you receive without reciprocating. Long-witness intimacy, where someone has watched you change over years. Priebe's argument is that several of these are mutually exclusive by structure, so routing them all through one partner is not demanding — it is impossible.
