Needs were met early without having to be exaggerated or fought for, so the adult expects the same ordinary reciprocation: I say what I need, you supply it if you can, and if you cannot you say so. The insecure styles find asking hard and receiving harder. Priebe notes the secure person also gives help readily, which is part of why asking feels unremarkable.
