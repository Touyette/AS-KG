The anxious partner gives constantly and feels unvalued, because what is given is the thing the other is allergic to. The avoidant partner keeps themselves level through everything life throws and feels unvalued too, because they are also expected to absorb someone else's crises. Priebe's observation is that in these pairings both people believe, accurately from where they stand, that they are carrying it.
