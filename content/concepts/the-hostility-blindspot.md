Adapting to a mildly hostile emotional environment means tuning out low-level hostility aimed at you — which reads, from inside, as resilience. The cost is a dulled sense of the hostility you emit. Priebe's point is that an anxious partner is exquisitely sensitive to contempt in tone and posture, so what looks like overreaction to your words is frequently an accurate reaction to something else entirely.
