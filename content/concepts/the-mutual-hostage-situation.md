Holding a negative view of self and of others at once produces a relationship the fearful-avoidant does not admire and cannot leave: they need someone to soothe the view of self, and their view of others means the partner is not respected or looked forward to. What holds it together is that each knows the other's worst material. Priebe notes it recurs across partners, because it is the intimacy itself that triggers the criticism.
