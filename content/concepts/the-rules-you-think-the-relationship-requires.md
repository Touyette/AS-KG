A set of rigid conditions learned early and now applied without inspection: I can never be angry with a partner. I must caretake at any cost. I cannot put anything between us. They kept you safe once, which is why they feel non-negotiable. Followed long enough, they hollow you out — which is why the avoidant spectrum often feels more alive single than partnered.
