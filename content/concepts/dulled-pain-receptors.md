Priebe's analogy for the avoidant end: being unable to feel physical pain looks like an advantage and is disabling, because pain is the alerting system — stop, this is damage, rest. Emotional pain does the same job. It flags when the thing you are doing is wrong for you and when someone is treating you badly, and a system that mutes it loses the warning, not just the discomfort.
