Priebe's worked example. A long-distance partner was going to visit; within days she was cataloguing everything wrong with them and losing attraction. What had actually happened was a rule switching on — while they are in my space I may not ask for time alone, refuse closeness, or have needs of my own — and the disgust was a response to that sentence, which her partner had never uttered. The rules were hers, and she had assigned them to him.
