The avoidant half of the working-model pair. Confidence settles on the self and withholds itself from everyone else, so the only arrangement that feels stable is one where nothing load-bearing depends on another person. It is not arrogance about one's worth so much as a settled expectation that other people will not be there at the moment it counts.
