Priebe's organising metaphor for the whole series. Security is a working immune system: bad things still happen, breakups still hurt, and you recover at an ordinary rate. It is not a life without difficulty — she is careful about that — it is the absence of a fundamentally distorted view of what relationships are. Insecure attachment is the compromised version of the same system.
