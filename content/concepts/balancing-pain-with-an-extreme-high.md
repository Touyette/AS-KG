The fearful-avoidant feels pain at anxious volume and co-regulates like an avoidant — which is to say, barely. Unable to sit with it and unwilling to be vulnerable about it, they offset it with something large: the sudden travel, the enormous ambition, the extravagant redirection. Priebe notes all insecure styles cope badly at times; what is specific here is having the anxious level of pain with the avoidant level of support.
