Fearful-avoidants arrive warm, present and genuinely interested, and are good at making people feel safe to open up — so a conversation can feel deeply connecting and, on reflection, contain almost nothing about them. Priebe notes this makes the style easy to mistake for secure at first meeting: no coldness, no frantic energy, and no disclosure either.
