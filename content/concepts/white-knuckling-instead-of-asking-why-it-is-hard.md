Priebe's objection to the standard advice, which she says attachment content actively reinforces. The anxious partner is told to give more space and grits their way through it while their body is on fire; the avoidant partner grits their way toward a commitment they have not examined. Both skip the question worth asking — what would I have to learn for this to feel bearable rather than survived?
