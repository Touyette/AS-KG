Seeing a partner withdraw, the anxious system reads it through its own machinery: if I withdrew, it would be to draw someone closer — so they must need love. And so love is delivered, in quantity, to someone who was asking for space. Priebe names the trap: this is how you can give continuously, receive nothing back, and conclude you are being undervalued, while never once giving the thing that was wanted.
