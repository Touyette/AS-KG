Why insecure conflict resists resolution. Her worked example is an argument about washing up in which one person is really arguing that they are worth loving and the other that they cannot be controlled. Settling who does the dishes is easy; settling a lifetime of wounding in one conversation is not, and the second is what is actually on the table.
