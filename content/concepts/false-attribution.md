A feeling that arrived earlier and unnoticed finds a plausible cause later in the day and fastens onto it. Her worked example runs from an unflattering photo in the morning to a partner's harmless like in the afternoon — on an ordinary day a flicker of irritation, on this one apparent proof. The logic can be wrong while the feeling is entirely real, and usually old.
