Secure people are better at leaving relationships that do not meet their needs, and Priebe's explanation is unsentimental: having other options, they can see clearly. Noticing that ninety per cent of the regulating is theirs and ten per cent is met, they read it as information about fit rather than as insult or injury — and act on it. Seeing your partner as the only route to relief is what removes that clarity.
