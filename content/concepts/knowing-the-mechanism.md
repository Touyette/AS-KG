Her answer to why understanding the origin matters at all, given that understanding changes nothing by itself. Without knowing where an impulse comes from you cannot anticipate it, so change has to be attempted blind and in the moment. Knowing the mechanism is what makes it possible to tell when the old response no longer fits the situation.
