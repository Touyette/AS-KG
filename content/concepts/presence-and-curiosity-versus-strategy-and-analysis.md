A child who was met with presence and curiosity copies it: they meet people as blank slates and let what is unique to each connection emerge. A child who was not has to find another route, and the route available is analysis — working out in advance who this person is, what they would like, and who to be for them. Both feel like paying close attention. Only one is attending to the actual person.
