Her framing for what an infant's emotional display actually is: the only means it has of purchasing care. Crying buys soothing, anger buys an injustice being noticed, fear buys protection. An avoidant childhood is one where that currency was consistently refused at the counter, so the child learned to obtain things by not offering it.
