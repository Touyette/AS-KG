The four-stage frame she maps healing onto: running the pattern without seeing it, running it while seeing it, being able to do otherwise with deliberate effort, and finally doing otherwise without effort. Most early signs of change sit in the second and third stages, which is why they feel unimpressive from inside — awareness arrives long before ease does.
