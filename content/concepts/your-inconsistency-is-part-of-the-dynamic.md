Weeks deactivated, then activated in an argument, then unsure whether you want the relationship at all — and no sense of what that does to the person on the other side. Priebe describes assuming it was her partner's job to be vulnerable first and their problem if they were not, before recognising that a partner's chronic dysregulation can be a reasonable response to never knowing which version of you arrives.
