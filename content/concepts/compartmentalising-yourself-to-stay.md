Entering a relationship having decided in advance which parts of you will not appear, on the grounds that the whole version would not be accepted. Priebe puts it in the harshest terms she uses anywhere: it tells the most vulnerable part of you that it can be traded for someone else's approval, and it works on self-esteem over years the way you would expect.
