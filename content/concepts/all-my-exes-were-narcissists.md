Priebe refuses this as an answer to what has gone wrong before — not because it is never true of any individual, but because as an account of a pattern it names the obstacle and then declines to work on it. If the partners keep turning out that way, the thing in the way is what you are drawn to, and that is the item on the list before the next relationship.
