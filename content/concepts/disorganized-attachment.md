The infant classification the fearful-avoidant adult style is correlated with. It was named for an absence rather than a presence: where avoidant infants consistently disengage and anxious infants consistently escalate, these infants showed no coherent strategy in either direction — moving toward the caregiver, then freezing or turning away.
