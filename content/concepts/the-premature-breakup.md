In an anxious-avoidant pairing neither person states needs or boundaries plainly, so unmet needs and unspoken expectations accumulate until someone — usually the avoidant-leaning one — ends it under overwhelm. The feelings are still there and the business is unfinished, which is exactly why the pair drifts back. The breakup solved the overwhelm and none of the causes.
