Seeing the problem clearly and having none of the equipment to solve it — which Priebe rates the most frustrating stage of the four, because the awareness arrives long before the capacity does. Its actual job is observation: when the triggering happens, what precedes it, which parts of life it reaches. People want to skip it and cannot.
