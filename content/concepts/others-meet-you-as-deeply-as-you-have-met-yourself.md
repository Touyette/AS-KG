Priebe names this as the most profound shift of her own attachment work: going from a bodily certainty of existential aloneness to a bodily belief that she could be known. The order mattered. Uncovering the no-go areas in her own psyche was what made it possible to communicate herself to other people at all — a thing she had previously experienced as impossible rather than difficult.
