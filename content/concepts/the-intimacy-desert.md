Priebe's name for the fearful-avoidant's supply problem. One relationship holds all of the intimacy, and everything outside it is desert. She describes living inside a warm house surrounded by a frigid world, where the heating sometimes cut out. The codependency does not come from weakness of character — it comes from there being nowhere else to go.
