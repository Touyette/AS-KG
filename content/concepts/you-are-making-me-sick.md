The fearful-avoidant takes the dose, has the reaction, and locates its cause in the person who supplied it — the love must have been wrong, because the need for it is real. Priebe says plainly that of all the styles this one is most likely to be emotionally abusive toward a partner, while insisting on both halves of her standard framing: the wiring was not chosen, and dismantling it is nonetheless the person's own responsibility.
