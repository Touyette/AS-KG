Insecure attachment tends to read self-improvement as raising your score on visible variables — fitness, money, being interesting. Priebe's claim is that those recruit people who also rank on those variables and do not know how to connect. What actually makes healthy love available is the capacity to stay regulated while holding someone else's complexity, including the parts of it that threaten you.
