Meeting a current situation with emotions belonging to an old one — furious that a partner cannot read a need that went unmet in childhood, or that they failed to give space you never asked for. Priebe's point is that most insecurely attached people do this continuously while taking the reaction as an accurate reading of the present, and that catching it is the whole of the first step.
