Security runs a continuous cycle: share the inner world, get feedback, update. Without it there are two failure modes. Feel something strong with no obvious external cause and conclude you are irrational — the feeling goes underground taking its information with it. Or feel something strong and assign it to whatever is nearest, so someone must be behaving badly. Priebe's example of an inner world that stopped updating is a friend still talking about an ex a year on as though the breakup were last week.
