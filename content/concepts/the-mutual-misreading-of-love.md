Two people each performing what they understand love to be, and each reading the other as failing to return it. One brings problems to be shared and hears refusal; the other keeps problems off the table and hears complaint. Priebe puts this at the top of the misunderstandings between the anxious and avoidant sides, because neither party is withholding — they are running incompatible definitions.
