A claim that cuts against the stereotype: the secure style feels emotion in its purest form. The anxious person feels very intensely and often inaccurately, half in a fantasy. The avoidant registers far less than is actually running, storing the rest out of awareness. Grief is grief for the secure person and loneliness is loneliness — painful, undisguised, and not accompanied by panic in either direction.
