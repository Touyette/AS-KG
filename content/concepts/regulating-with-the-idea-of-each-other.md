Priebe's account of why insecure couples produce enormous fights: both are relating to the picture of the other person, and to a picture of how a relationship should go, rather than to what is in front of them. Attunement is the corrective not because it prevents disagreement but because it catches divergence early — a chasm cannot open quietly between two people who are actually in the present moment together.
