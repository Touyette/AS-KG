Unable to take the medicine, the avoidant takes analgesics: overwork, drink, substances, food, compulsive escape into fiction. Priebe's objection runs through an Advil advert she saw — pain says you can't, Advil says you can — which she reads as exactly wrong. Pain is the indicator, not the problem. Silence it and you keep using the injured arm.
