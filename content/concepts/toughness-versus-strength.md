Attachment communities frequently read the avoidant style as the emotionally strongest, because it does not visibly suffer or ask. Priebe's counter is that it is the most durable, not the strongest: toughness absorbs pain without processing it, strength feels the appropriate thing at the appropriate time and makes a better decision because of it. The strongest style is secure.
