Attraction tracks recognition rather than benefit. If the dynamic you grew up inside required you to over-assert your needs or over-assert your boundaries, that is the dynamic that will read as love later, and you will keep selecting for it until the underlying adaptation changes. It is the reason anxious and avoidant people find each other so reliably.
