When the connection feels threatened, the anxious system stops seeing another adult with needs of their own and starts seeing a parent — someone to be placated by abandoning your position, or fought hard to be heard at all. Priebe is clear that conflict with people you love is dysregulating for anyone; the tell is the shift in who you think you are talking to.
