Wanting the major decisions of a life — where to live, what work, which experiences — to originate with you, partnered or not. From outside it can look like control. From inside the circumstances of the life are the fixed part and the relationship is the part that has to fit around them, and it may not occur to the person that others find deciding together calming rather than costly.
