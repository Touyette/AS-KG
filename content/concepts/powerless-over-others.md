The premise Priebe rates as the single most useful thing an anxious-spectrum person can accept. If you truly could not influence what someone thinks or feels about you, the whole repertoire becomes pointless — and what appears in its place is the set of questions you have been avoiding: is this the right person, is this situation safe for me, what would a better option look like?
