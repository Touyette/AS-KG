The assumption that the intensity of your feeling measures the badness of your situation. Priebe uses it to explain a pattern she sees constantly: an anxious person in an emotional flashback feels overwhelmingly awful, concludes the awfulness must be coming from the person in front of them, and reaches for toxic, gaslighter, narcissist. The positive version does the same work in reverse, and produces idealisation.
