Activated, the worldview is I am not okay and you are, so the responsibility for fixing this is yours. Deactivated, it inverts: I am fine, you cannot manage yourself, so the responsibility is mine and I should audit my own conduct. Being thrown between the two is disorienting precisely because each position feels complete while you are in it.
