The arithmetic that keeps the pattern running. A house with heating ten per cent of the time still beats an outdoors with none, so it is rational to stay inside and work on the boiler. Priebe's point is that the fixation on getting a dysregulated partner regulated again is not irrational attachment — it is the only option with a probability above zero, and the unconscious has done the maths.
