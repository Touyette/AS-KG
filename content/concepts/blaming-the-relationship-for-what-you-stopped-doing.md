Priebe's own repeated pattern: disciplined about health and work when single, and then, on entering any relationship, unconsciously avoiding all conflict and putting her own needs behind her partner's — until one day she looked across and concluded he was why her career had stalled. The partner had not asked for any of it. The abandonment was hers, and it made the fantasy of leaving look correct.
