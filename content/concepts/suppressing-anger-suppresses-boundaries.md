The part of you that registers a line being crossed and the part that gets angry are the same part. Put anger under a general ban — as an unsafe childhood teaches — and the boundary-setting faculty goes down with it. Priebe treats boundaries as what keeps the alive, engaged parts of a person present inside a relationship, so losing them is not a small administrative cost.
