The avoidant default is that any need brought to you is excessive and belongs to the person carrying it. Priebe's target is narrower than it sounds: ninety per cent of your needs really are yours to handle, and ten per cent are relationship business that two people put on the table and work out. Recognising that category exists at all is most of the change.
