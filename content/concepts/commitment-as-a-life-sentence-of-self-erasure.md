Priebe's reframe of avoidant commitment fear. If being in a relationship means obeying the rulebook, then committing means obeying it for life — and the resistance is accurate. Secure people commit easily not because they are braver but because they are not betraying themselves: when an arrangement stops working they renegotiate it. The fear is of the terms, not of the person.
