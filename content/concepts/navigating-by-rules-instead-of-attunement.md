Where the glue between people was never shared feeling, a child builds a rulebook instead: if she looks happy this morning, approach; if not, keep clear. It works, so it generalises — behaviour observed, patterns learned, risk of rejection minimised. The blindspot is not the method's accuracy but its omission: nothing in it involves being in emotional contact with anyone.
