A named feature of anxious attachment: a vivid picture of a future in which everything has come right — the family, the travelling, the ease — with no account of how anyone gets there from here. Priebe's correction is arithmetic. If conflict is unresolvable now, five years on it is unresolvable with children added, because the fantasy has no ground under it.
