A severely compromised system and an allergy to the only treatment. Priebe adds the detail that makes it worse: the painkillers do not hold for the fearful-avoidant the way they do for the dismissive. So the need surfaces anyway, the dose is taken, the reaction follows, and there is no version of the arrangement that works.
