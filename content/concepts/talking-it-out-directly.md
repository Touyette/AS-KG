The drama triangle is what you do when a need cannot be stated outright. Secure people are not carrying shame about having feelings and needs that differ from someone else's, so the direct conversation is simply available — say how you feel, ask how they feel, look for a resolution. Priebe is clear this does not mean it always works, only that it is not frightening to attempt.
