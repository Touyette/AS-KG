Her central coinage here. In law or debate, arguing in bad faith means knowingly pushing an agenda other than the stated one. In an insecure dynamic the same structure appears without the knowing: the real want is hidden from the person holding it. Functionally it behaves identically — the stated issue never resolves, because neither party is actually arguing about it.
