Priebe is unusually firm here: if someone's behaviour looks irrational to you, that is a statement about the depth of your understanding, not about them. Given their history, their biology and what is happening around them, what they are doing makes sense from where they stand. This does not make it right or well-informed — it makes it explicable, which is the only thing you can work with.
