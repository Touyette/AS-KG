The place a mixed-attachment conflict can end: accurate emotional information and an accurate account of events on the table at the same time. Reaching it costs both people their defence — the anxious partner has to say plainly what happened, the avoidant partner has to accept the feeling as real before the sequence is settled. Neither concession works unless the other is made.
