The principle she puts at the centre of the whole endeavour, and the one most often split in half. People collapse the two clauses together — if I did not cause it, then fixing it is not mine to do — and the framework becomes an explanation for staying as you are. Both halves have to be held at once: you did not choose the patterning, and you are the only one who can change it.
