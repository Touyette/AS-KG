A belief Priebe declines to simply refute. Stated as worth — nobody better would want me — it is false. Stated as skill — I do not yet have the equipment to relate to someone who communicates well — it is often accurate, and it names the actual work. The two readings feel identical from inside and lead to opposite conclusions.
