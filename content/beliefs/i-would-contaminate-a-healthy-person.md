The anxious style doubts its desirability while holding that it is fundamentally well-meaning with a lot of love to give. The avoidant holds that it is solid and has plenty to offer practically. The fearful-avoidant, seeing its own strategic and manipulative moves clearly, concludes that it is bad — and so rejects the person offering healthy love, to protect them from it.
