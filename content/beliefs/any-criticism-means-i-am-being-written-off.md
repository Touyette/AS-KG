Feedback can only be taken in by someone who trusts they are being seen whole — and if nobody has been shown the whole of you, nobody can. So a single note reads as: none of this is wanted. Priebe describes a support group whose members had seen each other vulnerable before giving feedback, and the first time her body registered criticism as care rather than as being written off.
