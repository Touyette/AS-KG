The deepest layer, and she says every insecure style holds some version of it. For avoidant patterning the shame attaches to having needs at all — without the accompanying knowledge that everyone has them and that secure people simply do not carry shame about it. Because a self-rejecting layer sits on top, someone else responding warmly is not enough to shift it.
