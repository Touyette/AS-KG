Priebe names this as a characteristic error of the anxious style: I meant well, therefore I did not do anything wrong, therefore if there is a problem it belongs to them — they are toxic, or a narcissist. The step that is missing is the ordinary one secure people make without strain: you can act on good intentions and still hurt someone, and the hurt is real either way.
