Her deepest layer, and the most uncomfortable. Where warmth and validation were genuinely owed and not given, a kind of childlike purity persists — a self-concept as endlessly giving, which has never had to absorb that good intentions still injure people. Intimacy requires setting that ego down, which is precisely why it is frightening.
