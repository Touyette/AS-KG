Every style carries a slightly wrong picture of what recovery looks like, and this is the fearful-avoidant one: the emotions and needs that break through are read as the malfunction, so healing means stopping them. Priebe's counter runs the other way — the route out is making attachment needs more conscious day to day, not less, which is precisely what feels wrong.
