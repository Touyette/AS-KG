The avoidant model of how love arrives: keep everything running well, and someone will turn up, admire the finished life, and that will be love. Priebe's correction is that secure love is two people seeing each other's difficulties and supporting each other through them — so a secure partner will come looking for where you struggle, which is precisely what the model has no place for.
