Learned in a family where needs were fused and personal limits were not permitted: in a relationship you may not have boundaries, and everything asked of you is owed, in exchange for love. Priebe notes the fearful-avoidant version has a release valve the anxious version lacks — outside a relationship, all of it is allowed again.
