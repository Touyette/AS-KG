Learned in infancy with a caregiver who came intermittently: the crying that finally worked cannot be allowed to stop, because stopping is what ended the attention last time. Picked up mid-cry, the baby often becomes harder rather than easier to settle. The adult version arrives at the point where a fight could resolve.
