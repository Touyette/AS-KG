The assumption that a serious apology has to come with a guarantee, and that anything less will not save the relationship. Priebe's objection is that a pattern laid down in infancy does not end after one bad night and ten minutes of reflection, so the guarantee is usually a lie — and a lie repeated across several apologies is what actually costs you the person's trust.
