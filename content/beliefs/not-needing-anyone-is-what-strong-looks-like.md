Strength read as self-sufficiency: needing nothing from anyone, never asking, handling it. Priebe separates appearing capable from being capable — the cost of the appearance is the perspective, feedback and complementary skill that only come from other people, plus the knowledge of what you do not know, which she treats as a component of competence rather than a concession.
