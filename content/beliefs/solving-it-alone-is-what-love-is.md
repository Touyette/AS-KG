Care defined as the absence of imposition. Keeping yourself in order so that nobody has to carry you is not experienced as withholding — it is the form love takes, and the effort behind it is largely invisible to everyone else. A partner who brings their difficulties to be handled together is therefore not asking for closeness; they are declining to do their half of it.
