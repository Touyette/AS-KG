Every child starts life assuming they are the cause of what happens around them. Most get that corrected by bringing their reading of events to someone older who can disconfirm it. Without co-regulation that correction never arrives, so the assumption survives into adulthood as a conviction that outcomes could not possibly have been shaped by anyone else.
