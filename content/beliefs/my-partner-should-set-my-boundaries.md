Her sharpest reading in this video: much of the anger anxious people direct at partners is, underneath, a complaint that the partner is not doing the boundary-setting on their behalf. If you are always saying yes, someone else has to be the one who says no — and the expectation is rarely stated, because it is not consciously held.
