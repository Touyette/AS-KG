Meeting a present situation with emotions belonging to an old one is the thing being caught, and catching it warrants congratulation rather than the self-attack that usually follows. Rushing straight to the apology is not repair but a fawn response, the fourth branch alongside fight, flight and freeze. What works instead is writing down the whole unfiltered account first, then apologising with the cost stated plainly and the unhealed wound named, so the other person has enough to draw their own boundaries. The least expected step is finding what need the unwanted behaviour meets.
