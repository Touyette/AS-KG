Anxious-avoidant breakups often arrive prematurely, with needs and expectations left unspoken until someone ends it under pressure. Reconciliation then runs on best behaviour: agreeing at the door to the amount of space that broke it last time, and deciding in advance which parts of oneself will not appear. The test set against that is whether a person responsible for a four-year-old would let her live with this partner, and what would have to be in place to keep her safe. A vivid future counts for little without a route from here to it.
