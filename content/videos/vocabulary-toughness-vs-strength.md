Attachment communities often read the avoidant style as the emotionally strongest, because it does not visibly suffer or ask. That reading misses unmetabolised grief, losses never felt because the system would not release them into awareness, and it misses the difference between setting a known need down intending to return to it and never registering the need at all. Emotion subtracted from a decision as irrational discards data; behaviour that looks irrational marks the limits of the observer's understanding. Toughness of this kind requires isolation, since give-and-take needs someone able to say what they need.
