Fearful-avoidance is not simply anxious triggers added to avoidant ones; it carries a layer of feeling about the oscillation itself. Because subtler emotion is out of contact for long stretches, a need surfaces late and hard, arriving with a deadline attached because it will shortly go back underground — and is then reviewed from the deactivated side with shame, the feeling disowned along with the episode. Wanting space carries its own guilt, since enmeshment patterning reads any move toward separateness as a withdrawal of care. The recovery fantasy is of eventually not being emotional at all.
