The organising belief is that love is earned by what a person provides, which turns worthiness into a standard that keeps moving as it is approached and leaves a bodily sense of falling just short. Needs go unstated at exactly the point where stating them feels most dangerous, on the unspoken arrangement that they can be raised once the relationship is secured. Idealisation follows from a negative view of self paired with a positive view of others.
