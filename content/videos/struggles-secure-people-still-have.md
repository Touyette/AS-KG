Security does not make other people show up differently, which is the expectation corrected first. What changes is internal: an inner secure base, which shows up as the capacity to recognise that what one needs is not on offer in a relationship and to stop, without the frantic push to get the other person to see it differently. Repair runs on the same footing, naming the harm and staying steady whether or not it is accepted. Excitement is still there, but chosen.
