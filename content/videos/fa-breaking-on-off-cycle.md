The on-and-off cycle rests on a boundary problem mistaken for a compatibility problem. Having never seen a limit held inside an intimate relationship, the adult concludes that partnership means total absorption, so ending it becomes the only available way to ask for space — which is why wanting to leave while keeping the other person in reach reads as a request rather than a decision. Afterwards the avoidant machinery reports that nothing is wrong, postponing the grief rather than cancelling it, and the intimacy desert makes the ex the only remaining supply.
