Avoidant regulation is not the pull to reach out being refused; it does not arise in the first place, helped by a life arranged so that mood stays predictable without anyone else involved. The stated fear is that intimacy will cost stability. Under it sits self-disgust at one's own need, visible in accounts of hardship shrunk past plausibility, and under that the conviction that something is wrong with oneself, which every insecure style holds some version of. Security arrived at by deliberate work is the model to look for, and no one opens another person.
