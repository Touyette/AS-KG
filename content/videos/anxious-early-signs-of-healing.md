Three markers that the anxious pattern is loosening, beginning with recognising the kind of person repeatedly pursued as a choice rather than a run of luck. Limerence is described as believing an assembled version of someone against the evidence about the actual person, with a wager underneath it: that things come permanently right if this particular person can be got to care in this particular way. Change shows as catching the invented version while it runs, and as an external locus of control starting to give way.
