Adapting to a mildly hostile emotional environment means low-level hostility stops registering, which from inside reads as resilience, hence the suggestion of watching a recorded conflict back with the sound off. Attachment is built partly by both people pursuing, and the asymmetry when only one does is invisible from inside, so it has to be counted rather than sensed. Judging a partner incapable of helping means problems get solved alone and handed over finished, on a model of love as the reward for a well-run life.
