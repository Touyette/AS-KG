Attunement is reading another person's state, letting one's own respond to it, reflecting it back accurately, and only then helping it settle. A child met with presence and curiosity copies that and meets new people as blank slates; where feedback about what maintains connection was unreliable, anything working seven times in ten deserved to be run every time, and rigidity followed. Analysis stands in for attunement, placing a person on the right axes rather than meeting them. Insecure couples generate enormous fights because each is relating to the idea of the other.
