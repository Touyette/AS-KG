Care that arrived sometimes and not others teaches a child that the signal was not loud enough, so the strategy becomes amplification — escalating anger on one channel, escalating vulnerability on the other — and unpredictability itself starts to register as what love feels like. Co-regulation gets learned without its other half, leaving the adult dependent on someone else to settle them and watching the bond continuously for withdrawal. Repair runs through naming a feeling as one's own rather than relocating it, and stating a need plainly.
