Nothing is being wrecked on purpose. A response that was exactly right for the environment which produced it is being applied to one it does not fit, and that is a reading error rather than a wish to ruin things — the two point at completely different work. The park is safe at three in the afternoon and not at three in the morning; the difficulty is that nobody taught you to tell the time. Earning security does not remove the danger, only the confusion about which hour it is.
