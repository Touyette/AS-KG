Conflict goes differently when the issue arrives as a cost both people are paying — the time, the energy, the argument that keeps recurring — rather than as a report of injury, and when each has said in advance what tends to happen to them under pressure. Underneath sits engulfment fear: the low-level expectation of being required entirely and left with nothing of oneself. Arriving with the answer already chosen buys compliance without agreement, and resentment behind it. Urgency is treated as a reading from the nervous system rather than from the problem.
