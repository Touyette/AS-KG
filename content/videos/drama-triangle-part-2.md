Each style is walked into its corner. Anxious conflict occupies the victim position and runs on “I will only be happy once you meet my needs” — a search for a parent rather than a partner, and one the literature calls controlling rather than merely deprived; the exits proposed are Codependents Anonymous and powerlessness over others. Avoidance takes the persecutor corner, mistaking practised detachment for boundaries. Fearful-avoidance ends in a mutual hostage situation: a relationship neither admired nor leavable, because the poor view of self needs soothing and the poor view of others rules out replacement.
