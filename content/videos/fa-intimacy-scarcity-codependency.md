Shame-bound material, which is to say ordinary human emotion, is kept out of every social and professional setting, so it surfaces only where self-control gives way — inside a romantic relationship. That leaves one person as the sole supply, and since a non-zero chance of being met beats none, the arrangement holds. It traces back to a childhood in which the only comfort available came from whoever was generating the distress. Widening the supply is described as a sequence: a paid witness first, then rooms where someone else goes first.
