The capacity that most determines whether close relationships can be sustained is the ability to stay in emotional discomfort. Defensiveness is fight-or-flight arriving inside a conversation, and it takes hold at the point where a disagreement stops being about its subject and becomes about whether one is a certain kind of person. A sense of self that the current conversation does not have to confirm is what makes genuine humility possible, alongside communities that expect amends. Self-esteem holds being well-meaning and being wrong at once.
