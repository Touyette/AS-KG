Boundaries fail differently on each side. Where the difficulty is anxious, the line is known and cannot be stood behind, because a limit that cannot be tolerated from the inside will not hold, which makes the first work internal rather than interpersonal. An avoidant system produces no clear statement of need at all — it produces overload and a judgement about the other person, and withdrawing without saying anything leaves the situation to recur with more force. Underneath sits a belief that having limits at all makes someone bad.
