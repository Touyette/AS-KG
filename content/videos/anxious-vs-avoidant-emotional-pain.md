The load-bearing variable underneath attachment is taken to be how emotional pain gets processed rather than how people relate. Avoidance is a misnomer, since avoiding something requires knowing it is there: attention reroutes to the solution the moment a difficult feeling appears, and pain receptors dulled early look like an advantage while disabling the system that says stop, this is damage. The anxious counterpart registers the pain fully and lacks the mechanism that ends it, having watched nobody handle their own distress. The fearful-avoidant carries both jobs.
