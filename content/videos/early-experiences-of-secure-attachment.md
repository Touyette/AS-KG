Security is produced by sensitive caregiving, which is emphatically not perfect caregiving. It depends on enough separation between parent and child that the child's emotional range is not experienced as a verdict on the parent — the failure modes being over-identification, where the state is taken personally, and under-identification, where practical provision counts as the whole job and warmth is not counted as a need. Containment means holding an experience the child cannot yet hold and handing back something usable. The result is more than one setting, and the capacity to admit having got it wrong.
