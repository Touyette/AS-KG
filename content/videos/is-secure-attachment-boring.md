The worry that security will feel flat gets treated as reasonable rather than naive. What insecure relating supplies is relief: the sharp high that arrives when a background sense of not being enough briefly lifts, which is easily mistaken for joy because nothing else reaches that intensity. Security offers something structurally different — being alright as a steady baseline rather than an event that has to keep recurring.
