Dysregulation shows up either as a reaction far larger than the present occasion warrants or as a diffuse offness with no locatable cause, and the biases split by style — one treats a feeling as weather to be waited out, the other judges a feeling mistaken when no defensible cause is available. Attunement is the missing capacity: noticing a state accurately, naming what is there rather than a verdict about it, and parking it deliberately with a real time to return.
