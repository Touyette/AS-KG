At the top of the scale two incompatible programmes run at once: get the anger out, and keep this person on side, since they are also the one who could bring the system back down. A child scapegoated for naming what the family would not discuss concludes that anger is itself proof of being irrational, and the adult meets each small irritation with an internal ruling that it does not warrant a response. Withheld that way, it returns as a reaction wildly out of scale with the incident and exactly in scale with the arrears.
