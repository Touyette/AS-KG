Activation is read as an emotional flashback rather than a mood, which makes the age of the distressed part the useful question to put to it. The tactics that enforce a claim on someone's attention are legitimate at four and ruinous later: fawning, dressing a want as a principle about relationships, turning the volume up on the pain. Each feels like vulnerability in the moment and each manages the other person's response, and the success they buy is a relationship someone does not authentically want to be in.
