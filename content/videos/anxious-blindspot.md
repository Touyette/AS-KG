The anxious wheel is deflated on one side: a real openness to being taught and changed by loving someone, with no matching self-protection. Much of the anger directed at partners turns out to be a complaint that they are not doing the boundary-setting on the anxious person's behalf. Reflection after each ending narrows the list of traits to avoid, so successive relationships contain less of the bad without ever becoming the good one. The counter-exercise is to plan a life on the certainty that the relationship never arrives.
