Below the stated account of what a relationship is for sits an unconscious premise, and each style brings a different one: a partner who will supply worth, a life already designed that someone must slot into, or closeness that never touches the shame. Conflict then runs on accidental bad faith, where the surface grievance is sincerely meant and is not the operative one. The most legible symptom is friends knowing more about the private verdicts on a partner than the partner does. Stated criteria come from the conscious mind; past choices come from underneath it.
