Attachment styles do not behave like personality types, and the habits carried over from personality systems distort them in two opposite directions. One misuse learns another person's style in order to route around what they have plainly said they want; the other treats a diagnosis as licence rather than a place to start. The principle holding the middle — not a person's fault, still their responsibility — is the one most often split in half, each misuse keeping whichever side suits it.
