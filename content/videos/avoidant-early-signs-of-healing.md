The fear that keeps avoidant people out of the work is close to universal among them: that attending to feeling means being permanently at its mercy. Movement shows in the opposite handling, with emotion entered into a decision as a data point rather than obeyed or corrected, and an explanation for someone's behaviour held alongside the impact it had without either dissolving the other. The position being left assumes competence on one side and deficiency on the other; the one replacing it assumes everyone is behaving reasonably given what they have been through.
