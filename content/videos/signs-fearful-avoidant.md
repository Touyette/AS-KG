The pattern is read from motivation rather than behaviour, and it is not anxious and avoidant traits stacked together: distorted emotion and distorted reasoning are both available, but never at the same moment. Relationships are scanned continuously for power and for what each person is withholding, on the premise that people betray each other whenever it makes sense to. Commitments made sincerely by one configuration come due to another, which is where sincerity and unreliability stop contradicting each other.
