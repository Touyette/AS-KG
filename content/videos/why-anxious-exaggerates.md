One side of an argument is organised emotionally and the other temporally, which is why the same event reads so differently from each. Under activation the anxious system treats transmitting the feeling as the only route to relief, so the story gets turned up until the listener arrives at the right emotion, and the listener answers by auditing the timeline for gaps and contradictions. The middle ground has accurate emotional information and an accurate account of events on the table at once, which starts with the feeling and the need said directly.
