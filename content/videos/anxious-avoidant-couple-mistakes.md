Ten recurring failures in the anxious-avoidant pairing, most of them traceable to expectations neither person ever said out loud. Resentment gets treated as a verdict rather than a prompt to ask what was expected and whether it was ever stated, raw feeling gets fitted with a manufactured rationale because feeling alone will not be taken seriously, and an apology arrives only to be met with the ten items still outstanding. The stoicism that first attracted one partner turns out to be the avoidant patterning, and moving toward security costs the existing balance of power.
