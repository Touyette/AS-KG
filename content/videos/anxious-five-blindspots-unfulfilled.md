Five places where the anxious system misreads its own situation. Accommodation registers as the route to closeness and functions as a barrier to it, and when connection feels threatened the partner stops being another adult with needs and becomes a parent to be placated or fought. Permanent openness leaves nothing in the way, so a short reply or a distracted evening lands as a verdict; some self-protection is what allows a partner to be human. The widest blindspot is which people keep getting chosen in the first place.
