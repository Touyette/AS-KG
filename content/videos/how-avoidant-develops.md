An infant's emotional display is the only currency it has for purchasing care. Where reaching out in need reliably drew hostility or withdrawal, the brain stops attending to feelings that buy nothing, and the adult version rationalises a feeling away whenever the surrounding circumstances do not obviously justify it. Emotions that were met with disgust become shameful to have, which is why displayed vulnerability in others can provoke such strong repulsion — the repulsion works as a map of which feelings were rejected first.
