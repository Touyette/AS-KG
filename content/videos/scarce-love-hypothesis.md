The familiar claim is that insecure people mistakenly believe love is scarce and cling to whoever currently offers it. The correction turns on a distinction between the soup and the bowl: love is plentiful and freely given, and what differs is whether a person arrived with the capacity to hold any of it. That reframes improvement as a relational skill rather than a higher score on visible variables like fitness, money or being interesting.
