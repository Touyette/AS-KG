Rigid conditions learned early — that anger at a partner is never permissible, say — get applied to adult relationships without inspection, and since the part that registers a line being crossed is the part that gets angry, suppressing one suppresses the other. Deactivation can then be a response to those private rules rather than to the partner, which is why commitment can feel like a sentence of self-erasure. Blame flips with the state, each side certain the repair belongs to the other. Complex causality is the way out.
