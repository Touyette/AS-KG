Security is not the absence of conflict; disagreement is a normal part of living alongside another person, and what distinguishes it is that arguments are not loaded with older material that keeps resolution out of reach. Co-regulation runs toward independence rather than rescue, with each partner able to take a turn as the steady one and to offer what the other actually needs. The quality named as most important is expectations a relationship can realistically carry.
