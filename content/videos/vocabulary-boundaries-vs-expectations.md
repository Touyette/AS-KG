A boundary is a decision about one's own action, enforceable whatever anyone else chooses; an expectation is something wanted from another person. Expectations are not the flaw in a relationship, since relationships run on them, but they function only once stated aloud and actually agreed to. A limit phrased as one's own response can come with a side door: asking what need drove the behaviour and offering a workable route to it. Disengaging without ever stating a limit is not a boundary, though avoidant patterning often counts it as strength.
