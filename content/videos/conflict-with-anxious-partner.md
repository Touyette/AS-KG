Under pressure, anxious patterning trusts the feeling and arranges the reasoning to fit it, while avoidant patterning trusts the reasoning and arranges the feeling to fit that, which is why acknowledging the feeling rather than contesting the logic is rated the most useful move available. A feeling that arrived earlier and unnoticed fastens onto a plausible cause later in the day, and old grievances turn up mid-conflict as further evidence. Naming the conversation as a problem to be resolved, and giving a specific time, settles what an open-ended promise cannot.
