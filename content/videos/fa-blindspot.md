The fearful-avoidant blind spot is structurally unlike the other two, because the task is integration rather than addition. Most people on this spectrum spend more time in one response than the other and build an identity on it — the analytical one, the selflessly giving one — while carrying quiet embarrassment about the half kept out of view. Reading a past relationship as the place where the self was lost, then entering the next determined to suppress the unwanted side, is what guarantees the repetition.
