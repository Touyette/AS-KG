The name misleads: to dismiss or to avoid implies something present in awareness to be dismissed, and the characteristic move is that the need never reaches awareness at all. From the inside the style reads as competence — problems get solved, practical care gets given, whatever does surface does get dealt with — which is why it is so often mistaken for security. What defines it is everything falling outside that: needs filed as wants, hurt processed alone, and love understood as the absence of imposition.
