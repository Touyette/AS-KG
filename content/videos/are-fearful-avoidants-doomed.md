An insecure strategy is defined compactly as whatever was learned to get a need met without stating the need, laid down at an age when manipulation is not yet possible. Two accounts of fearful-avoidant development sit side by side: one base strategy with trauma triggers that flip it into its opposite, and an early situation where the source of danger and the only available protection were the same person. The chaotic areas of a life are the easy ones, since the pain supplies motivation; the harder question is where the strategy is still succeeding.
