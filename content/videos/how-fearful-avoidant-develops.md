When the person supplying everything a child needs is also the source of danger, both attachment responses run at once: pursue closeness, never settle into it. Activation therefore peaks when things are going well rather than during conflict, which sets up an oscillation — leaving genuinely calms the system, distance revives the longing, and returning restores the conditions that started it. Crittenden's Dynamic Maturation Model reads the pattern as reasoning and emotion both available but never online together, one strategy running while the other is offline.
