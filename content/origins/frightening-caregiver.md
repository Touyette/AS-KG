The condition in which the person who supplies everything you need to survive is also the source of danger. Her framing is that this need not be literal lethal threat — a caregiver who periodically loses control of themselves is, to an infant, an enormous animal in the room whose behaviour cannot be predicted, and the child has no coherent answer to whether approaching is safe.
