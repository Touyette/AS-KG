The child who breaks the unspoken rule that the family's dysfunction is not discussed, and is called crazy, irrational or over-reacting for it. Priebe suspects this background sits behind most fearful-avoidant development: a healthy response to injustice gets pathologised until the child concludes that any anger it feels is evidence of its own malfunction.
