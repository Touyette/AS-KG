A caregiver who cannot tolerate a feeling in themselves reflects back something other than what the child is having — meeting sadness with insistent cheerfulness, well meant and inaccurate. Since children learn what they are feeling from how it is named for them, the result is lasting uncertainty about one's own internal states.
