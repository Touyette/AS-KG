Why insecure attachment runs in families without anyone intending it. A parent can only teach a child to metabolise the emotions they can metabolise themselves; the ones they cannot get quietly rejected, often by a parent convinced they are being fully available. Her example is a parent whose own shame fires whenever their child gets angry.
