The avoidant over-inflated wheel: learning early that understanding why something happened mattered more than processing how it felt, and getting very good at it. A finished relationship gets reviewed as a sequence — this interaction produced that fight, my move produced their counter-move — often with real accuracy about one's own part. She rates the skill highly and says secure people use it constantly; the problem is running it alone.
