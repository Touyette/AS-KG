After a hurt, withdrawing to work the feeling out privately and returning only once it has been settled — so the injury itself is never brought to the person who caused it. It can feel like consideration, dealing with your own state before involving anyone else, and there is something in that. The cost is that the hurts that mattered most are the ones never raised.
