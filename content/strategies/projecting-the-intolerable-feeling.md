When a feeling cannot be processed — shame above all — it is relocated to someone else, because a feeling that belongs to another person is one they can be recruited to fix. Her example: the self-disgust in the mirror becomes a search for proof the partner finds you disgusting. It is not experienced as invention; it feels like accurate knowledge of what they think.
