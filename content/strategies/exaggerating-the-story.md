Turning up the events so the listener will arrive at the right feeling. Priebe is explicit that this is not deception in the ordinary sense: the emotion being reported is real and undistorted, and the facts are being bent in service of transmitting it. Which is also why calling it out as exaggeration reliably produces more of it.
