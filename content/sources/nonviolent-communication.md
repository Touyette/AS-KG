The system Priebe recommends specifically to avoidant-spectrum people learning to state a boundary: it works by going underneath irritation or overwhelm to the unmet need beneath, and by phrasing it so the other person can actually respond. Her argument is pragmatic — telling someone their feelings are unwarranted, even where true, does not produce the outcome you wanted.
