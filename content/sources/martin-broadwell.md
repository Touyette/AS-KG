Credited with the first description of the four-stage learning model in 1969, later restated by Curtis and Warren and popularised by Noel Burch at Gordon Training International as the four stages of learning any new skill. Priebe traces the attribution herself and notes the model was never intended for psychotherapy — she applies it because attachment healing is a learning process.
