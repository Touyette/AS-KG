Not in the moment — afterwards. Look back at the times you froze and withdrew, or went furious, or went scattered and absent, or went into caretaking, and ask what relational need was live underneath. Priebe presents these responses as the body working hard to keep the need out of your awareness, which makes them the most reliable index of what is there.
