Her prescription for the lean-avoidant version: a daily practice of dropping attention out of the head and into the body to find what is actually there, then saying it somewhere designed to be safe — therapy, a support group, or a friend with whom the commitment is mutual. She is realistic that going from never sharing to sharing openly is too large a step to take at once.
