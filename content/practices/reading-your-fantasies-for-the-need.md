Ask not what happens in the fantasy but what you feel in it. Priebe works her own — singing a sad song to a crowd who sing it back, despite not being able to sing — and lands on emotional mirroring: something painful wants to be seen and reflected so she feels less alone. The move is then to find which relationship that need belongs in and ask for it there, rather than leaving it to be met in imagination.
