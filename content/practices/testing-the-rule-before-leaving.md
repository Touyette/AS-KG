Before ending a relationship that has gone empty, name the rule you have been obeying and find out whether it is real. Priebe describes doing exactly this — laying out her aversion to setting boundaries in her own space, and her fear that doing so made her a bad person — and finding her partner carrying an equivalent set of invisible rules. She came out of it more attracted to him than before.
