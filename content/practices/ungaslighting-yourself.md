Before treating a feeling as distortion, asking what it is accurately observing. Priebe's objection to the standard reassurance is that being told a fear is all in your head, when the fear is measuring a real barrier, teaches you to stop trusting your own instrument. The move is to separate the part that is a real read from the part that is an old wound colouring a new situation — and to keep both.
