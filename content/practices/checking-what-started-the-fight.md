At the end of a conflict, ask what began it and whether it was ever dealt with. Priebe's diagnosis of recurring fights is that the defensive responses generate so many secondary problems — the shutting down, the insults, the days spent on those — that the original issue is lost. An unresolved issue returns, which is why the same fight keeps arriving.
