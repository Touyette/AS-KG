Put the question to the distressed part directly: how old do you think I am? Priebe found the answer was rarely a competent woman in her thirties — it was a small child with no means of looking after herself. Naming the number is what makes the gap visible, and the work that follows is bringing your actual age back into the body rather than arguing with the feeling.
