When a partner's mood registers as rejection, Priebe pictures the hurt part of herself as a child, takes its hand, and tells it plainly that this is the other person having a hard day and that nothing is wrong with it — then goes and does something else, returning to the relationship for repair once both people are in a better state. It works because the instinct to protect a child is one most people already have.
