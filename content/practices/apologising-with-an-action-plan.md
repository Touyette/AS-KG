Express the remorse, say plainly what you can see it cost them — and then name the wound that is still unhealed, so the other person has enough of the picture to draw their own boundaries. Priebe calls this an adult thing to do rather than a shameful one, and says people can tell when they are being handed the truth, which is why it settles them more than a promise does.
