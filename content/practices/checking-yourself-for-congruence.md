Catch the small dishonesties: fine when you are not fine, of course I'm not mad when your body is broadcasting otherwise. Priebe suggests a gradient rather than a switch — ten per cent more congruent, then twenty, then thirty. The reason is mechanical: people cannot attune to what you are hiding, so incongruence is what keeps the feedback you never got as a child from arriving now.
