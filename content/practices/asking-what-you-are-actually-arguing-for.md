In the middle of a conflict, ask what you are so intent on securing that you would sacrifice either your partner or yourself to get it. Her examples are unflattering by design — preserving their picture of you as good, or keeping decision-making control — and that discomfort is the point, since these are exactly the wants nobody states aloud.
