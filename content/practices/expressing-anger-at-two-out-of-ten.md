Deliberately raising things while they are still minor, on the reasoning that smallness is the reason to speak rather than the reason to stay quiet. It requires overriding the standing veto every time, and it feels absurd — Priebe says the frustration of doing it is real, and that it is nonetheless the route to anger arriving in the situations that actually produced it.
