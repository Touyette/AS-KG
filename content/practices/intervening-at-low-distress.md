Treat your own distress as worth acting on around three to six out of ten, while options are still available. Her analogy is shopping for food before you are hungry: at a moderate level you can try a conversation, a limit, a change of approach and see how each lands; at eight or nine you are no longer choosing between options, only seeking relief.
