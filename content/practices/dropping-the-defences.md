Her own account, and the most concrete instruction in the video. Noticing the defences coming up as raised fists — the internal cataloguing of everything the other person has wrong — and deliberately letting them drop, so the words arrive. She describes coming away feeling beaten up, still convinced the accusations were untrue, and clear for the first time that being spoken to that way by someone she loved hurt regardless of its accuracy.
