Priebe is blunt that the fix is not better boundaries with other people. It is learning what a boundary violation feels like in your body, acquiring the self-soothing to sit inside the boundary once it is set, and committing to yourself that you will leave situations where you are treated badly. Self-soothing is hard here because being alone with yourself brings the self-hatred up, which is where inner-child work comes in.
