Relational wounds do need laying out — but not to someone who is having a hard time of their own in the same conversation. Priebe's move is to go somewhere else for the regulation first: another person, time alone, therapy. Then return to the conflict able to say what the issue actually was, rather than doing whatever it takes to be liked again.
