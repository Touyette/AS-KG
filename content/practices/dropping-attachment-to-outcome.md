A conversation entered with a hope — that they like you, that they say a particular thing, that it lands somewhere specific — is a conversation being steered rather than had. Priebe's instruction is to notice the script running, let it run without following it, and get authentically curious about what the other person is actually saying. You learn far more, far faster.
