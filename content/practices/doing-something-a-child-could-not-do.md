Write the list while calm: things a seven-year-old could not possibly do. Then when the flashback arrives, do one. Priebe's are deliberately mundane — clear the accounting, sort the tax, cook a proper adult meal, buy your own clothes, ring someone about a project without mentioning the conflict. The point is not distraction; it is physical evidence handed to a part of you that has the wrong number.
