The first thing after recognising a bad reaction is usually self-attack, and Priebe argues the opposite is warranted. Acting badly without knowing it is where you were; acting badly and knowing it is a different position, and doing otherwise is only reachable from here. She notes what is being given up in the trade — righteous anger is terrible for the soul and excellent for the ego.
