Priebe's question before re-entering a relationship: if you were responsible for a four-year-old, would you let her live with this person — and if so, what would have to be in place to keep her safe through the fights and the unavailable stretches? A clear no means the inner parenting to survive that dynamic is not there yet, whatever you decide about the person.
