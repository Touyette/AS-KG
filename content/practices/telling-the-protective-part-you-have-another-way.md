Being present feels threatening because a part of you is insisting that the strategy is what keeps you from being abandoned, and that this present-moment business has nothing to offer. Priebe's move is to sit with that part rather than override it — to say that there is now another way of getting the same needs met, and that it is not being abandoned in the process.
