Priebe's own worked case: triggered, on a train home early, texted that her partner could not meet her — and rage. Instead of turning the dial up she asked what she would do if there were no partner at all. A bath, a friend, some writing, a long sleep, a coffee out in the morning. Only once those options were visible could she go back and say honestly what was happening and propose meeting the next day instead.
