Do not end a relationship unless you are prepared to cut contact completely — Priebe suggests six months as the benchmark. Willingness to do that is the test: if you cannot, what you are feeling is overwhelm rather than a decision, and the work is inside the relationship. She is direct that staying friends without firm internal boundaries is unfair to the other person and to yourself.
