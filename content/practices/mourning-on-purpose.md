Six months with no return to the ex and no replacement — a new relationship over an open wound is a dressing that stops it closing. Let the birthday arrive, and the album, and the morning nobody brings coffee, and feel each one pass. Priebe keeps a document of everything she would say to a former partner, thanks and anger both, and still adds to it, which processes the feeling without disturbing their peace.
