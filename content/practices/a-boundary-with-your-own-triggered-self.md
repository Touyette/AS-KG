Boundaries you set from a regulated state about a state you will not be in. Priebe's own is leaving the room once anger passes a known threshold — read, run, do anything physiological — rather than trying to solve the problem through the person. She keeps it imperfectly and says imperfectly has still changed her close relationships materially.
