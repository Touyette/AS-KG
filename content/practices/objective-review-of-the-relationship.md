The counterpart practice: deliberately looking at the relationship from outside the fantasy — journalling, inviting feedback from friends less invested than you, keeping a running practical account of what you are looking for and whether this matches. She notes deactivation is harder to catch than activation because it pulls attention out of the body rather than into it.
