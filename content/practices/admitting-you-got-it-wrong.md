Her clearest picture of why security does not require competence. A guide who admits not knowing and goes to find out beats one who takes the question as an insult; and a guide who comes back to say the directions were wrong beats one who insists they were right and suggests your sense of direction is the problem. The second leaves the child's reading of reality intact; the third damages it.
