Check in repeatedly over time, recording what you feel alongside what you were doing, until the pairings become visible. The payoff she describes is deflating in a useful way: what lifts a low mood usually turns out to be sleep, daylight and a conversation rather than anything commensurate with how complicated the feeling seemed.
