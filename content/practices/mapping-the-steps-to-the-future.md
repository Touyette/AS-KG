Instead of the picture, the route: name the problems in the way, name what would have to change starting now, and raise both with the partner. What you are testing is not whether they agree to the destination but whether they will work the steps. Priebe is clear a secure relationship does not need the ending settled in advance — it needs both people clear about which step they are on.
