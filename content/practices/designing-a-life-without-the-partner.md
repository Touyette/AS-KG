Her exercise, and a demanding one: plan your life on the certainty that the relationship is never coming. What you would do with your time, which friendships and family you would invest in, what you would build, what you would take up with the hours currently spent tracking someone else. The point is not resignation — it is that boundaries need something to enclose. Her image is being handed fencing materials without knowing where your land ends.
