Telling the activated part that it has real information, that now is the wrong moment, and that this will be handled tomorrow — then handling it. Priebe is blunt about the second half: if you do not follow through, you will not believe yourself the next time, and the urgency will be correct. She describes her own version, having the hard conversations the next day, and the triggers losing their emergency quality as a result.
