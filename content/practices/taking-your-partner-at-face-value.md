Presenting your own position without editing and receiving theirs without decoding it. She draws a hard line here: if the only way you can get your needs met is by obscuring, exaggerating or managing your partner, the secure move is not better technique but not being that close to that person — and the same holds if you feel you must handle them as too fragile or unequal to hear the truth.
