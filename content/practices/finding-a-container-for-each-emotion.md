Priebe lists her own, and the specificity is the point: a somatic therapist and the radical honesty community for anger, authentic relating for intimacy, Tantra for sexuality, Buddhism for grief and staying present with pain. Cognitive understanding is not what changes these — being in the feeling with someone trained to hold it is. She describes anger reliably producing a pain flare or an urge to drink until that changed.
