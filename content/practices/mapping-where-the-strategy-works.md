Going through the areas of your life — relationship, work, family — and naming where the insecure strategy is currently succeeding, with no obligation to change any of it yet. Priebe frames it as putting an asterisk beside those situations and as honouring what the strategy did for your survival, on the grounds that you cannot dismantle something you have not yet seen working.
