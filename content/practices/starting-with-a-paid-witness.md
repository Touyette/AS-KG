Priebe's own sequence, over years: for the first year, one intimate relationship and nothing else; then a therapist, where being honest could be practised with someone trained to receive it; then, after about a year of that, one deeply honest friendship; then the skill generalised to new friends and communities. The order matters — the first step is with someone whose job is to not flinch.
