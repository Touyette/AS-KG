With the other person's consent, record a conflict and watch it back muted — or sit in front of a mirror during one, which is clumsier but works. The question is what emotion the body language alone conveys, and how someone sensitive to contempt would receive it. Priebe offers it as the most direct route to information you cannot get from inside your own head.
