The work the soup metaphor points at, and it differs by style. The anxious spectrum builds a relationship with itself so that incoming care has somewhere to land rather than being consumed on contact. The avoidant learns to tell safe from unsafe accurately enough to stop treating all of it as suspect. Priebe is direct that this usually needs professional help and takes a long time.
