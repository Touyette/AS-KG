Treat resentment as a prompt rather than a verdict: what am I expecting here, and have I ever made it explicit? Priebe suggests raising it lightly — admitting you have been waiting to be mind-read, and asking whether they are up for it. She notes what surfaces on the way: fear of rejection, and shame at having wants at all, which is where the healing actually happens.
