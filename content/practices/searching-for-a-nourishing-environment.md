A mentor's line Priebe says changed her life: you are used to thinking of your environment as emotionally impoverished, so you stopped looking for evidence otherwise — and healing requires believing there are ways it could nourish you, then going out to find them. The cruelty of the wound is that the thing needed to heal it, mentorship from the environment, is exactly what it tells you is unavailable.
