Say beforehand what tends to happen to you in conflict and what you will need if it does — a pause, a restatement of what you are both aiming at. She counters a common worry directly: admitting you have feelings does not repel an avoidant partner, because they already know. What overwhelms is strong emotion arriving with no shape to it, and a stated plan gives it shape.
