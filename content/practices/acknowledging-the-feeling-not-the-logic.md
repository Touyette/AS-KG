The central move, and she rates it the most useful thing in the video. Grant the feeling fully and hypothetically — if that were what was happening, of course it would hurt, it would hurt me too — then say plainly what is actually the case. Most of what you say is about the emotion; a small part addresses the facts. Disputing the reasoning first is heard as denying that the feeling is real.
