Take what you feel as a data point worth entering into the decision rather than a verdict to obey or a fault to correct. Not following every impulse and not discarding logic — simply having more information than before. She points out the cost of leaving it out: plans made without the emotional cost accounted for are where a lot of burnout comes from.
