Telling safe people in advance that you are learning to raise small things, and that if something sounds trivial it is because you are trying to catch it early rather than because it is secretly enormous. Priebe adds the in-the-moment version too — saying outright that this is a two out of ten and feels silly to mention. Both remove the need for the thing to be big enough to justify itself.
