Say in advance that what you are about to do is difficult for you and that you may be unsteady around it. Priebe pairs this with the harder half — coaching yourself that a sensitive area does not oblige your partner to respond perfectly; they are also entitled to their own reaction. She describes having to go back days later to say plainly that something needed more attention than it got.
